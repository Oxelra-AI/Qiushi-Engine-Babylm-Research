# Changelog

## Initial public release — 10 September 2026

- Chinese and English technical reports with editable LaTeX sources, a shared
  bibliography, eight vector figures and common numerical tables.
- Two final model packages, tokenizers and loading implementations; model
  weights are distributed through Git LFS.
- Three-stage research guides and 74 topic entries connecting scientific
  questions, methods, experiments, results and research notes.
- Original training and mechanism programs, configurations, data constructors,
  available checkpoints and complete evaluation records.
- Counterexamples, conditional findings and scientific corrections retained
  alongside their supporting evidence.
- Reconstruction, build, validation and packaging instructions.
- Apache-2.0 for original code; CC BY 4.0 for reports, figures and written
  research documentation; original terms retained for third-party materials.

The report date remains 8 September 2026. Research scores and model weights are
unchanged by release preparation. See the [release contents](reproducibility/STATUS.md)
for the material scope and verification summary.
