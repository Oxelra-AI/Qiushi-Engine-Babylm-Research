# Figures and Typography

Retain the existing report design; repository reorganization does not require a new cover or main figure.

- Preserve the original Qiushi Engine mark, author order and centered title.
- Use editable vector figures: TikZ for structures and interventions, and Python/Matplotlib for numerical comparisons.
- Use a white background, dark gray text and restrained blue-gray and purple accents. Keep each method's color consistent across figures; color must not replace labels.
- The Chinese and English reports share scientific figures with English labels; captions follow the language of the report. Use TeX Gyre Heros throughout the figures. Check font sizes at the final embedded dimensions rather than shrinking the entire figure to resolve crowding.
- Data figures must identify metrics, units and comparison conditions, with explicitly defined error measures. Do not depict method comparisons as inevitable improvement over time.
- The overview focuses on the three scientific stages.
- Organize it as scientific question, three research stages and core outcomes, without a logo, promotional heading or file listing. Retain text and model schematics, and use a hierarchical tree to show parallel exploration in several directions. Do not add gating, blocking or winning-path symbols, or use node counts to represent experiment counts.
- Highlight only the two successive frontier models, data-efficient learning principles and open research materials. Keep the full material catalog in the text or repository navigation, not inside the figure.
- Draw the overview at a final width of 160 mm with 9, 8 and 7 pt type. Performance claims refer to the recorded leaderboard snapshot; material availability must reflect the actual release scope.
- Preserve component-level declines, conditions of applicability and retractions. Visual improvements must not change experimental facts.
- Captions first identify the research subject, then define necessary visual encodings, comparison conditions and statistics. Use formal, direct language without repeated explanations of drawing choices, unrelated release notes or defensive asides.
- Before release, inspect the rendered PDF for overlap, clipping, whitespace, font embedding and caption correspondence, not just the source code.

Submission templates belong to a separate manuscript project. This technical report does not claim to satisfy every production requirement of a particular journal or conference. The mark identifies the team; the code license does not automatically authorize its use in other products.
