# Evolution of the Research Interpretation

This page organizes the research lineage by scientific decisions. Chapter order is not evidence that every judgment was fixed before the experiments.

| Prior result or question | Revised scientific judgment | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compact text ranks differently early and late in training | Text value may depend on budget allocation and learning stage | Fixed-budget replacement and late-stage comparisons |
| Coherent material and shuffled fragments perform differently | Content exposure must be distinguished from joint availability of related information | Repetition, restatement, wrong-source and split-window controls |
| Familiar behavior recovers while unseen-query performance remains weak | Performance recovery alone does not establish that a capability remains reusable | Supervision allocation and internal functional interventions |
| Dense masking remains effective with sparse targets held fixed | Input conditions and prediction-target counts require separate tests | Sparse/dense masking and supervision comparisons |
| New learning conflicts with preservation settings | Effective constraint strength and the conditions under which it acts must be measured together | Ordinary-input preservation, gradient diagnostics and full nine-component evaluation |

These traceable design changes connect the three research stages. Any experiment claimed to test a strictly prior prediction still requires records of the frozen design, experiment start and first measurements. This page is not a retrospectively added preregistration.
