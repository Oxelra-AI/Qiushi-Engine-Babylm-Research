## Reference interpretation

- If broad ex-Entity `D1_VminusB` is near zero when the breadth ladder lands, then the old positive ex-Entity `V-C` is not evidence for a companion mechanism; it mainly sits in `B-C`/stream composition or geometry.
- If broad ex-Entity `D1_VminusB` is substantially positive across families/checkpoints, then within-packet source-related re-expression becomes the mechanism-bearing quantity. The matched clean controls then locate that mechanism relative to clean experience, but do not replace it.
- `D1_VminusCmax` and `D2_VminusCmax` are absolute placements under matched row/update geometry. They can confirm a useful total stream effect, but a positive total with `V-B≈0` would not support source-conditioned companions.
- The permuted-companion arm becomes high-value only if broad `V-B` survives, because it equalizes compact-rewrite fertility and multiset properties that B cannot match while breaking own-source correspondence.
