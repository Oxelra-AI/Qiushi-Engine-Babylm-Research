## Qwen-Derived Content

The 12,236 `qwen_pair_packed` rows contain packed sequences where each sequence holds one or more original–rewrite segment pairs. The original text comes from the permitted BabyLM corpus; the rewrite is generated by Qwen (an approved teacher model under BabyLM Strict-Small rules, ≤9B parameters). These are derivatives of the permitted corpus, not externally sourced data.

The 3,005 `cleanqwen_fineweb_compact_view_reinvest` rows are FineWeb-derived text that has been processed through Qwen and compact-view reinvestment. FineWeb is distributed under ODC-By.

## Exposure Budget

- **Stage I representative model** (Qiushi-Engine-Frontier-Advancement): cumulative exposure approximately 86M words across repeated passes through the pool, well within the 100M word budget.
- **Stage III representative model** (Qiushi-Engine-Principle-Guided-Frontier-Advancement): 80 continuation updates consuming 3,162,742 acquisition words and 517,332 preservation-counted words; conservative total cumulative exposure 89,685,369 words, below the 100M word budget.
