**Stage III representative model** from the three-stage BabyLM Strict-Small research program: Frontier Advancement → Principle Discovery → Principle-Guided Frontier Advancement.

Produced by the Qiushi Engine autonomous research system.

- **Parameters**: 36,458,592 (MLM head) / 36,210,368 (encoder only), including 995,584 private adapter parameters across 48 tensors

- **Replication**: 42.232 at seed 62065 (same method, different continuation randomness)

## Method

Stage III applies three principles discovered in Stage II:

1. **Dense second-view masking**: In packed original–rewrite training rows, the rewrite view's content groups are densely masked (132,283 groups, 176,607 tokens at seed 62064), removing within-view completion evidence. The original view remains fully visible.
2. **Sparse credit assignment**: Prediction loss falls only on a deterministic subset of the masked positions (21,479 label groups, 28,590 tokens), making the visible cross-view correspondence the cheapest sufficient explanation for those targets.
3. **Ordinary-mask parent preservation**: A frozen copy of the Stage I model provides KL matching on ordinary 15% whole-word masking positions, constraining drift where the teacher's predictions are evidence-supported, while leaving the dense acquisition view free.

Only the 995,584 private adapter parameters are updated; the 35.2M backbone and slow adapter parameters remain frozen throughout.
