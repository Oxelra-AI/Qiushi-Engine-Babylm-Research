Therefore the learned k0 versus k16 question is not formal identifiability alone; it is whether the neural learner selects the intended assignment rather than a lower-complexity shortcut.
- If single-relation bridge conditions formally identify all held relations through the held-held comparison graph, but learned transfer stays relation-local, then the limiting factor is failure to use the comparison graph/role interface, not absence of symbolic information.
