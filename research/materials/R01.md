# R01 - Sparse routing and prefix memory

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Sparse routing and prefix-average memory were compared with capacity-matched and shared-core dense controls across text pools and seeds. Neither showed a stable general advantage. Tied and untied output heads define different controls, and prefix averaging is distinct from writable entity-state memory.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
