# R02 - Character, subword, and morphological pathways

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Character, n-gram and morphological pathways were compared with lookup and dense controls to test compositional representation. Their effects varied by task, with parameter count and tokenization contributing to the comparison. These pathways differ from an explicitly supervised character-equality matcher.
