# R03 - Whole-word and token masking

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Paired whole-word and token-masking comparisons across two seeds found a task-dependent effect rather than universal superiority. Whole-word masking improved BLiMP, EWoK and Entity in both short-budget comparisons, while Supplement, COMPS and Reading did not show consistent gains.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
