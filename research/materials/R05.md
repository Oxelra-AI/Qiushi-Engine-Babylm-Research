# R05 - Recursive autoregressive training

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Recursive autoregressive training was reproduced on the permitted corpus and examined through tokenizer and Reading controls. Correcting the leading-space boundary narrowed but did not close the Reading discrepancy between public weights and local reproduction. The remaining difference cannot be attributed to corpus choice alone.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
