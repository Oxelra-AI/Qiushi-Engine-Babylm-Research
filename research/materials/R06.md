# R06 - Vocabulary, depth, width, and budget

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Vocabulary, depth and width change compression, embedding capacity and training cost together. Retraining with a budget-compliant tokenizer defines a different comparison from earlier studies using a larger-corpus tokenizer. Depth comparisons have completed endpoints; the earlier tokenizer results do not establish admissible small-data model performance.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
