# R07 - Frequency gating and fine-grained representations

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Exploratory construction**

Support-gated compositional residual embeddings test whether fine-grained composition improves on ordinary lookup embeddings. The zero-gate model matched the reference, but the trained nonzero-gate endpoint did not improve it. The ordinary embedding term remains present at the large-gate limit; other gates and seeds remain untested candidates.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
