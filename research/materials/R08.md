**Experimental result**

Mature-recipe comparisons examined depth, width, vocabulary, LAMB optimization, sequence length and masking schedules. Component-specific contrasts and completed two-stage recipes answer different questions: a favorable bundled recipe does not identify the contribution of every component changed within it.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
