# R10 - Aligned reformulation and correspondence

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Aligned simplification and reformulation were compared with wrong correspondence, source matching, exact repetition and separated presentation. WikiAuto, SynCSE and clean generated reformulations define different data comparisons. Their effects depend on task and control condition, and correcting AoA units changes the interpretation of earlier aggregate scores.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
