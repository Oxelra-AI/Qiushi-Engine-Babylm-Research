[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Modeling method**

Compact-view reinvestment shortens a second expression of each source and spends the saved budget on additional passage pairs. Pair count rises from 10,094 to 12,155 within a 423,520-word replacement block: about 20.4% more passage pairs in that block, not more documents across the entire collection. Compression, additional coverage and candidate admission are distinct parts of the construction.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
