# R12 - Adjacency, repetition, and presentation layout

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Compact views, exact repetition and broken adjacency test the contribution of local pairing, while block and interleaved breadth layouts test presentation. These interventions have different task and item effects and share a historical tokenizer boundary. Their contrast is not interchangeable with the later target-deletion experiment.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
