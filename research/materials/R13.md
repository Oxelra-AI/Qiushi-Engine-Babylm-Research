# R13 - Extractive compression and bridging text

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Exploratory construction**

Balanced and wider extraction, together with source-attested fluent bridges, test whether coverage can be increased while preserving lexical and semantic fidelity. Prototype checks establish construction properties, not successful large-scale training with fluent bridges; lexical closure alone does not establish faithful meaning.

Report section: `sec:experience_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
