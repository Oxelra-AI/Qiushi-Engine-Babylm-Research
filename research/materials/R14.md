**Conditional result**

A completed deterministic rule-rewrite screen compared correct pairing, same-entity wrong pairing, source-only and shuffled controls. The four conditions produced task trade-offs rather than a joint improvement in the intended abilities. Token-aware row matching did not equalize every condition's visible-token exposure.
