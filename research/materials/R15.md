# R15 - Entity-mention consistency

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Entity-mention consistency was tested against both wrong-pair training and ordinary whole-word masking. A second seed and corrected decoy analysis weakened the broad-benefit interpretation. An advantage over destructive mismatches does not by itself establish useful consistency learning beyond ordinary prediction.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
