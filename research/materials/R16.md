# R16 - Counterfactual and cross-span supervision

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Counterfactual propagation and cross-span supervision test newly acquired source dependencies separately from sensitivity already present in the model. Dependent, nondependent, local, wrong-source and absent-source controls distinguish these possibilities. Short training did not establish general relation acquisition.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
