# R17 - State text and directed masking

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

State-coherent stories, relation-directed whole-word masking and evidence-visible or inverse-priority masking sometimes improved targeted behavior while imposing broader language costs. Near-identical early checkpoint tables have unresolved identity limitations and do not establish independently verified developmental trajectories.

Report section: `sec:guided`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
