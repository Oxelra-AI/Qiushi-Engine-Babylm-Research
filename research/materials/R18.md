# R18 - Auxiliary causal objectives

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Whole-batch causal replacement and gradient-calibrated shifted-token auxiliaries are distinct ways of combining causal and masked prediction. Completed full-model evaluation did not establish improvement over the corresponding reference. Near-aligned local gradients were therefore insufficient evidence of a useful combined objective.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
