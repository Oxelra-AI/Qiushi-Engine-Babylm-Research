# R19 - Self-sampled replaced-token detection

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Self-sampled replaced-token detection with detached sampling and embedding-gradient decoupling completed a 20M-word screen with task-dependent benefits and costs. Majority-class shortcuts and balanced detector performance are distinct tests of the auxiliary task. A delayed mature-model detection objective remains a proposal without a completed efficacy result.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
