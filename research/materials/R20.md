# R20 - Target weighting and supervision coverage

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Word-mean losses, support floors, source-absent target weighting and directed cross-view masking test how prediction credit is allocated. Better fit on the selected targets can coexist with lower broad scores. Exact-swap target matching passed construction and short implementation checks but lacks a trained efficacy result.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
