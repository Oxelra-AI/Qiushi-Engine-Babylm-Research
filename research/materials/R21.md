# R21 - Relational pivots and counterfactual cues

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Pivot targets, sparsely calibrated auxiliary losses and spatial witnesses test whether supervision selects a relation or merely retrieves a visible answer. Matched supervision mass and erased or permuted controls distinguish those mechanisms. Low candidate yield and saturated retrieval performance limit claims of learned state selection.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
