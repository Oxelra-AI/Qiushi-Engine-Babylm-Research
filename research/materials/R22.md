# R22 - Gradient exchange and projection

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Exploratory construction**

Centered four-condition exchange gradients and projected or row-centered alignment measure local relationships between learning signals. Same-row alternative negatives and gradient-norm calibration define the comparison. Orthogonality or a fitted alignment coefficient does not establish long-term learning benefit or retention.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
