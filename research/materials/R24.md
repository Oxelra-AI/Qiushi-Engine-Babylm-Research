# R24 - Adaptive masking granularity

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Adaptive masking and late switches from whole-word to token masking have stage-dependent effects that can reverse during training. Paired data-by-mask comparisons and complete trajectories show why an intermediate advantage need not survive the final budget, particularly when sequence and batch geometry differ.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
