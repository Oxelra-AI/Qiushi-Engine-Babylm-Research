# R25 - Data order and developmental curricula

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Same-content ordering, cadence and first-pass developmental curricula test different aspects of training experience. Prefix-truncation repairs also change the material actually seen, so a bundled recipe cannot isolate an ordering advantage. Occurrence-timing studies provide a separate, shorter-budget test of age-of-acquisition behavior.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
