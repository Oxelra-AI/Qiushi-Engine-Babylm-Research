# R26 - Context-length caps

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Context-cap comparisons hold pair dose fixed while changing training geometry. A large historical Overall difference was largely driven by the reference's negative AoA contribution, whereas the seven-family mean did not improve. The result is not a broad, simultaneous gain across language tasks.
