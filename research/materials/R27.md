# R27 - Optimizers and mature model states

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

AdamW, LAMB and Muon comparisons, trust-ratio probes and mature-model switches depend on horizon and optimizer state. One LAMB configuration also changed other model settings and is not a single-optimizer ablation. An incomplete low-learning-rate condition does not provide an exposure-matched 20M-word comparison.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
