# R28 - Checkpoint averaging and restarts

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Stochastic weight averaging, adjacent-checkpoint averaging, tail restarts and another training seed distinguish checkpoint selection from a reproducible stabilization effect. Evaluated averages did not reliably exceed the selected checkpoint, and different sample panels could favor different exposure points.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
