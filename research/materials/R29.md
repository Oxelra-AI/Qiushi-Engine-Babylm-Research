# R29 - Relation dose and learning maturity

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Binding-dose and role-switch studies compare mature and weak bases, concentrated and sparse mixtures, and natural continuation. A learnable synthetic task does not establish acquisition from admissible natural-text pretraining. Additional diagnostic exposure and the nonneutral role-fixed comparator limit attribution to relation dose alone.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
