# R31 - Zero-initialized bottleneck branch

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Modeling method**

A zero-output bottleneck residual preserves the initial model function and permits disabled-path controls, including bounded training-parity checks. Joint training still updates the base parameters. Initialization or short-horizon parity therefore does not imply a frozen-base trajectory throughout training.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
