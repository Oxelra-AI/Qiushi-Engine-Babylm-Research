# R32 - Main and auxiliary path separation

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Joint residual training, dual-view auxiliaries and main-disabled/auxiliary-enabled paths assign gradients to different parameters. Path-specific checks and item gains and losses showed that early recovery of difficult examples did not guarantee broad improvement or transfer to a mature model.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
