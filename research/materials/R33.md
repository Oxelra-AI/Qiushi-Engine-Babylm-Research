# R33 - Dedicated increments on a frozen base

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Modeling method**

Frozen-parent continuation learns a dedicated increment using ordinary prediction and KL while the parent weights remain fixed. Coherent and span-broken replay form the closest matched pair; ordinary and shuffled alternatives differ in additional respects. Disabling the increment restores the parent structurally, but retention with it enabled is an empirical outcome.

Report section: `sec:frontier`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
