# R34 - Model lineage and scale selection

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Stock compact models, continuous packing, jointly trained residuals and frozen-parent increments form separate branches rather than an additive ablation sequence. Repaired adapter-aware full evaluation defines the released-model comparison; older scores describe historical measurement conditions. Selecting an inference scale after evaluation changes the function but does not create another training replicate.

Report section: `sec:lineage`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
