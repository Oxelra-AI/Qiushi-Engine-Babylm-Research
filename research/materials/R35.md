# R35 - Writing and reading with supplied addresses

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Supplied-address writable memory was tested with overwrites, distractors, slot interventions and eventwise address corruption. Consistent address renaming preserves the addressing relation and is a symmetry control. These functional results are distinct from the withdrawn query-independent tiny-memory task and do not establish learned routing from raw text.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
