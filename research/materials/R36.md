# R36 - Answer shortcuts in a tiny binding task

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Withdrawn**

The early tiny-memory binding interpretation is withdrawn: answers followed action outcomes regardless of the queried entity. High accuracy and sensitivity to write permutation therefore do not establish query-conditioned binding. A corrected corpus and training script were constructed, but no completed successful corrected-model experiment is established.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
