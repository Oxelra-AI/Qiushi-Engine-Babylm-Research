# R37 - Routing from natural text to addresses

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Raw, lexical, recurrent and discrete routers test whether text can supply the addresses used by a state-memory mechanism. In one router, 90.4% token accuracy concealed zero query recall; predicted routing failed where supplied routing retained functional effects. This interface failure differs from the withdrawn answer-generator shortcut.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
