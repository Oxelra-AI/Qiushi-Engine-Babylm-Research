# R38 - Pointer and copy initialization

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Exploratory construction**

Pointer and embedding-factorization initialization activated a copy pathway, but the repaired candidate-token readout still failed to select a property according to the queried entity. Active copying is therefore distinct from learned entity-property binding. This failure is separate from the query-independent answer shortcut in the withdrawn tiny-memory task.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
