# R39 - Format preadaptation and label alignment

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Format acquisition and inference-time tolerance test different aspects of reading. Aligned, permuted and disjoint-label preparation all helped a later task, weakening the claim that a particular learned address correspondence was essential. The comparison does not establish a universal head-only explanation.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
