# R40 - Frozen readers and within-group selection

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

A frozen reader with a four-candidate group-softmax selector approached supplied-address performance on exact names and role swaps. Selecting prior versus revised states remained weaker, and stable examples inflated pooled accuracy. Explicit tags, candidate sets and direct supervision are conditions of the result.

Report section: `sec:memory_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
