# R41 - Identifiability of relational rules

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Initial-owner changes, alias balance and independent event or ranking reversals expose anti-copy shortcuts compatible with high training accuracy. These controls test whether the intended state-transition rule is identifiable. Success on a separate relation graph does not resolve ambiguity in the earlier task.

Report section: `sec:anchors`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
