# R42 - Binary relation graphs and sparse anchors

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Binary relation graphs with sparse orientation anchors support sign propagation through shared representations. A three-seed core result is complemented by shared, untied and fully tied comparisons, anchor reversal and relation-closure tests. Supplied candidates, incomplete control fits and changed example counts after edge removal bound stronger necessity claims.

Report section: `sec:anchors`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
