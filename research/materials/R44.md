**Exploratory construction**

Neutral and reversed graph packets and program-structured tasks test controlled relational data construction. Low clean yield, residual answer priors and leakage or type checks limit the prototypes. Scorer comparisons without verified model loading do not establish a confirmed functional mechanism.

Report section: `sec:anchors`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
