# R47 - Views, breadth, and repetition under a fixed budget

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Fixed-budget view, breadth, repetition and clean-data substitutions estimate treatment effects specific to model architecture and learning stage. Late-window comparisons use different token budgets and task populations across architectures. Increased sentence coverage is not necessarily increased independent-document coverage, and incompatible populations do not define one pooled effect.

Report section: `sec:experience_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
