# R48 - Register substitution and data-value prediction

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Register substitution holds admitted content fixed while varying the displaced material, making opportunity cost part of data value. Word-level and profile-level Jensen-Shannon divergence predictors have different corrected definitions; mismatched windows or task sets do not yield a valid prediction error. Second-seed and in-corpus measurements extend the comparison without establishing a universal predictor.

Report section: `sec:experience_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
