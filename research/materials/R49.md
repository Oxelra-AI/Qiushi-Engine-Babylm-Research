# R49 - Selecting explicit relational text

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Exploratory construction**

Relation-explicit FineWeb selection and natural state or spatial filters test whether usable relational examples can be identified from text. Their quality checks and short screens found limited clean yield, not a validated large-scale relation-training stream. Low yield under these filters does not imply that relations are intrinsically absent from natural text.

Report section: `sec:experience_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
