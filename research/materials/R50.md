# R50 - Deletion of source-absent and copied targets

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Deleting source-absent versus copied targets holds input fixed with nearly matched supervision. Local source-pair-disjoint effects are positive, but stricter document-disjoint intervals cross zero, and target-loss and seven-task rankings differ. These comparisons do not identify mediation fractions or share an estimand with the earlier compact-view triangle.

Report section: `sec:principles`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
