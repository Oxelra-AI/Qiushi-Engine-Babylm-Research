# R51 - Correct, wrong, and missing sources

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

True, wrong and missing-source interventions hold target tokens fixed to measure source-conditioned prediction. Large reformulation advantages also occur without direct supervision of the source-absent target channel. This separates the readout from that training channel but does not establish input-only learning or a causal explanation of final Overall.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
