**Methods and diagnostics**

Source presence, prefix placement, gaps, order and window position change different aspects of available context. Text-only coverage counts can be established independently of model loading. Historical likelihood contrasts with unresolved loader identity do not provide verified evidence of a model mechanism.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
