# R53 - Ordered composition and dynamic binding

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Ordered dynamic and static tasks distinguish decoding local intermediate states from composing a final answer. Intermediate information could be accessible while final composition failed. Poorly fitted from-scratch controls and failed readouts do not establish architectural impossibility; training fit, candidate priors and held-symbol behavior constrain that inference.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
