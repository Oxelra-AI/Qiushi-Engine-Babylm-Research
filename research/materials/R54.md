# R54 - Contextual hidden states and interventions

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Same-entity history replacement and factorial activation interventions distinguish representational change from functional state use. Topic or entity information can affect hidden states without producing the correct state-dependent answer. Null interventions delimit particular pathways rather than every possible contextual computation.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
