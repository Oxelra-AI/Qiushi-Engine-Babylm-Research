# R55 - State updates in microworlds

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Micro-worlds separate explicit state statements, action inference, contradictory evidence and target-word-free overwriting. They provide controlled diagnostics but did not become validated selectors of natural-benchmark performance. These tests are distinct from the withdrawn tiny-memory claim of successful compositional binding.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
