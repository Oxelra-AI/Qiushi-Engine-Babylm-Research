# R56 - Gradient conflict and learning-signal estimation

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Gradient conflict, transfer gain per word and within-experience credit estimators were tested with blocked permutations, same-bag and cross-pair controls, and held objects. Local associations did not become stable prospective predictors of the value of additional learning experience.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
