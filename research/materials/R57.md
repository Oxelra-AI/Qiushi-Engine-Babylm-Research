# R57 - Contrastive prediction and auxiliary readouts

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Contrastive predictive coding and strict continuation objectives were examined with same-source, cross-source and difficult-negative readouts. Auxiliary fit or high retrieval accuracy could improve without establishing newly acquired identity-specific context use. Successful discrimination of an auxiliary task is not equivalent to learning the intended dependence.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
