# R58 - Layer-wise readouts and position editing

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Layer mixing, diagonal-bilinear and ridge readouts, and local position edits test accessible information and transfer. Training fit coexisted with held-condition failures, while rotated-null controls constrained the interpretation. A small morphology-heavy edit set does not establish a general circuit-level intervention result.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
