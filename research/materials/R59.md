# R59 - Score changes under pair swaps

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Antisymmetric paired scores and cross-corpus contrasts test correspondence using category, length, target-swap and wrong-pair controls. Algebraic cancellation in a score and discrimination among existing models do not establish functional use of the relation or reliable selection of a better candidate model.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
