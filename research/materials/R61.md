# R61 - Macro averages and item-level changes

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Macro averages, gained and lost items, and oracle complementarity use different denominators and can disagree in sign. Item transitions and corrected percentage units explain why a higher task mean need not mean more examples answered correctly. A label-assisted oracle union is not a learned selector.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
