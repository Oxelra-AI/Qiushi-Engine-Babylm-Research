# R62 - Transfer conditions and representation geometry

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Conditional result**

Cross-architecture transfer contrasts, baseline-conditioned slopes, seed differences and centered principal-component analyses did not establish a capability-conservation law. Shared-item matching defines the comparison, while unavailable loadings and limited seed variation constrain interpretation. An observed difference between two seeds is not a population noise estimate.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
