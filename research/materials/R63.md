# R63 - Complete evaluation and model interfaces

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Complete evaluation depends on the full loaded model, task populations, AoA checkpoint trajectories and Reading or GlobalPIQA scoring semantics. Encoder-loader repairs can change the function evaluated. Missing measurements, measured zero, historical public displays and repaired local scores therefore represent distinct evidence conditions.

Report appendix: `app:evaluation`. The experiments and analyses below provide the detailed measurement records.
