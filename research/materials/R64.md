**Methods and diagnostics**

Word-boundary packing, intact paired views, truncation and continuous-window visibility relate counted words to the tokens and targets actually seen. The U256 packing experiment is a separate branch, not an assumed ancestor of the final model. Corpus origin and the teacher used to generate reformulations are part of the definition of the training experience.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
