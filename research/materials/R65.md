# R65 - Microbatches and gradient equivalence

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Methods and diagnostics**

Target-count-weighted microbatch loss corrects the unequal weighting produced by averaging means over different numbers of targets. Deterministic reduction tests, activation-checkpoint guards, evaluator-batching checks and short trajectory comparisons establish bounded implementation properties, not identical stochastic training or a measured full-training speedup.

Report section: `sec:measurement_branch`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
