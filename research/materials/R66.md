**Methods and diagnostics**

Custom-model reconstruction depends on matching weights, configuration, model code and branch controls. Same-seed tensor reconstruction differs from new-seed replication and exact training resume. Parameter count, word exposure, updates, trainer time and total search or evaluation cost measure different resources; unavailable costs are not zero.

Report section: `app:repro`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
