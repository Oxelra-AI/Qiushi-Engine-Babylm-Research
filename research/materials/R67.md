# R67 - Selectivity in relation learning

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Across three training seeds, exact recurrence and aligned reformulation produced opposite changes in true-source advantage on compact targets without source-token overlap. A neutral ordinary-text context anchors the comparison. The result identifies relation-selective learning in this setting, not a universal harm from repetition.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
