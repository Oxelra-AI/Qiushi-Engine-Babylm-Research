# R68 - Prediction windows with matched materials

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Row-split controls retain each recurrence or reformulation condition's material while moving source and companion outside the same prediction window. Two-seed neutral-anchor measurements show attenuation of the large source effects. Content matching holds within each local-versus-split pair, not between the recurrence and reformulation corpora.
