# R69 - Transfer conditions for natural reformulations

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Natural remention, Wikipedia/Simple-English and source-recurring versus source-absent targets reveal transfer that depends on relation and register. Wrong correspondence, exact copy and half-view dose controls delimit the effects. Separated-coexistence and exact row-split conditions differ in packing and are not interchangeable locality controls.

Report section: `sec:relation`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
