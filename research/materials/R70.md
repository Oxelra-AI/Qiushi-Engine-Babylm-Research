# R70 - Familiar performance and functional access with unseen symbols

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Query-first binding tasks compare answer-focused preparation, ordinary full continuation, static credit and interleaving. Familiar-symbol performance can recover without recovery on held query symbols. Donor/recipient interventions and fitted-direction tests constrain the explanation within the controlled task, without establishing general natural-language transfer.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
