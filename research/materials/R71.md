# R71 - Erasing and relocating functional signals

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Centering, zeroing, rotation and donor replacement of an attribute-position component demonstrate its functional involvement in a controlled binding task. Refitted directions did not restore held-symbol transfer. The result neither excludes every distributed recoding nor establishes that all downstream computation remained intact.

Report section: `sec:reach`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
