# R72 - Dense inputs and sparse supervision

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Modeling method**

Sparse-input/sparse-target, dense-input/sparse-target and dense-input/dense-target conditions separate input corruption from focused supervision on existing text. Both complete dense-input/sparse-target continuations exceed matched ordinary continuation at equal exposure. Ordinary continuation also differs in target policy, and the sparse-input/sparse-target control lacks a complete nine-task endpoint.

Report section: `sec:guided`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
