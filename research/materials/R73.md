# R73 - Preservation inputs and effective strength

[All topics](../materials.md) | [Reading guide](../reading_paths.md) | [Scientific synthesis](../scientific_guide.md)

**Experimental result**

Ordinary-state and dense-state preservation differ in input, target support and effective gradient strength. The KL is teacher-to-student; deterministic and random-state controls separate drift from stochastic disagreement. The common-position dense-to-ordinary gradient ratio is 11.835, so equal coefficients do not match strength. Attenuation and rollback explain part of the recovery.

Report section: `sec:guided`. Interpret historical claims within their stated conditions and in light of later corrections.
