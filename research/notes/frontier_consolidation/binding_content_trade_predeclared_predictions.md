## Mechanism now being tested

The common-window dose table and the family leave-one-out table are better explained as a fixed-budget competence-allocation mechanism than as uniform semantic scaling. Increasing the fraction of the 10M pool converted into same-window source plus companion packets raises repeated exposure to source records. Exact duplicate recurrence spends that extra exposure on the same surface string and increasingly displaces independent content. Source-conditioned compact re-expression keeps the same record addressable across a changed surface form: the model gets another route to bind entities, roles, and state changes while avoiding some of the value loss of exact duplication. The cost is that companion words and source-packet rows consume budget that could have carried distinct world-content examples, so EWoK-style world-content contrasts can fall while Entity Tracking rises.

The early packet-atomicity result is load-bearing for this mechanism: if the source and its compact re-expression are not in the same training window, the second view no longer gives a useful record-level contrast for entity/state re-identification.
