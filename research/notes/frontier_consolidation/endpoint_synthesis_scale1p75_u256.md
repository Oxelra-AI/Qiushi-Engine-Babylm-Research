## What U256 decided

U256 tested whether making all legally charged row-tail tokens visible under the same corpus/tokenizer/model/objective/optimizer would mature into broad transferable competence. The 20M screen was very strong (+0.9179 cheap7), but the 100M endpoint resolves that early signal as mostly non-maturing redistribution:

- 20M BLiMP/Supplement/GlobalPIQA gains did not survive broadly to the endpoint.
- Endpoint gains are mainly BLiMP (+0.9465) and Entity (+1.0323).
- Reading lost -0.8153, Supplement -0.3180, COMPS -0.3629, and EWoK is flat/slightly negative.
- SuperGLUE is only +0.0948, far too small to compensate the weak cheap surface.
- Lower final MLM loss and large weight-space trajectory redirection (u256 endpoint mechanism) did not imply official competence gain.

Mechanistic interpretation remains: U256 changes credit flow through source-tail visibility, mostly CHILDES/SimpleWiki suffix credit, not compact-pair content. The 100M score says that extra tail visibility alone is not the missing general data-efficient learning principle.

## Route implication

Both major natural endpoint routes after earlier analysis are now complete and below the target:

- scale1.75: strongest current legal endpoint, but still -0.2293 below 41.8; fixed residual branch amplitude closes as a SOTA endpoint route.
- U256: far below scale1.75 and only +0.0715 over spatial repair route status; faithful row-tail visibility closes as a SOTA endpoint route.

The protected substrate remains legal compact semantic second views plus reinvested diversity. The unresolved problem is not lack of lower MLM loss, not simple extra token visibility, not static residual capacity, and not naive complementarity. The next expensive work should not be another local variant of scale amplitude, U256 chunking, innovation masking, RTD, Muon, LAMB, or tokenizer size. The next scientific decision should be a high-level route judgment using these complete endpoint failures to reconstruct a mechanism that can improve broad transfer while preserving EWoK material/spatial/quantitative behavior, Supplement subject-aux inversion, Reading, and SuperGLUE.

## U256 intermediate trajectory screen launched

The complete 20M and 100M U256 points leave a trajectory ambiguity: did U256 never mature broadly, or did it peak and then forget/overwrite useful competence? Because this can change whether checkpoint selection or consolidation is scientifically relevant, a lowest-cost checkpoint screen was launched, not a new training route:

- U256 `chck_80M` cheap screen A on GPU0 for BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, output `data/u256_80m_cheap_screen/partA/`.
- U256 `chck_80M` cheap screen B on GPU1 for COMPS, GlobalPIQA_parallel, GlobalPIQA_nonparallel, Reading, output `data/u256_80m_cheap_screen/partB/`.

Scientific purpose: if U256 80M cheap7 is not near the 41.8-compatible cheap level (~43.7) and remains below scale1.75, no full U256 checkpoint evaluation is justified and U256 closes as non-maturing/forgetting. If 80M is unexpectedly strong, a full SuperGLUE/AoA check may be justified before route reconstruction. No additional pretraining route should start until this cheap screen is consumed and high-level route judgment is made.
