## Why run another GPU experiment now

The detached private experiment design/131 from-scratch sparse20 separated panel already changed the early-state evidence: separated aligned cheap7 is `40.292857142857144`, beating spatial repair route status 20M by `+0.6292857142857144`, beating the exact dualview panel readiness and budget `mlm_only` scaffold by about `+0.5064`, and beating separated shuffled (`40.06357142857143`) by `+0.2292857142857132`. Fragile columns are not damaged in the broad dual-view pattern: aligned vs spatial repair route status has Supplement `+3.15`, EWoK `+0.04`, Reading `-0.195`; aligned vs shuffled has BLiMP `+0.70`, Supplement `+1.15`, Entity `+0.45`, Reading `+0.205`, with small EWoK/COMPS tradeoffs. This is enough to justify a mature-state test of the same mechanism, but not enough to spend the full remaining `17,987,505` legal words because Muon, U256, and scale1.75 all changed character after early promise.

The short frozen-tail comparison will decide whether to continue, stop, or rebuild the private-pathway route:

- continue toward a longer tail only if true-aligned private learning at the mature 82M state improves source-free auxiliary NLL relative to shuffled and preserves the protected cheap7 surface;
- stop this exact route if aligned fails to beat shuffled/source-free base or damages the protected BLiMP/Supplement/EWoK/Reading/GlobalPIQA balance;
- rebuild the objective or optimization if source-free NLL improves locally but broad competence drops.

## Minimum reliable run chosen

I will use a `4,000,000` charged-word tail prefix rather than the full `17,987,505`. This is a small reversible branch (~22.2% of the remaining allowance, ~4% of the total 100M cap), but long enough to expose thousands of sparse top20 auxiliary targets from the dense pair region immediately after the verified 82M boundary. The official-compatible cheap7 evaluation cost dominates once a checkpoint exists, so a much shorter 1M–2M tail risks producing too little private learning for the same evaluation cost.
