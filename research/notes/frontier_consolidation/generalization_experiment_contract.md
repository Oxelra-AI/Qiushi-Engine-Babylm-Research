## Why the current dense runs are only local contrasts

Two asset freeze and principles trainings are running under the exact same legal compact-view reinvest corpus, tokenizer, DeBERTa-v2 8x480 backbone, WWM objective, optimizer, 100M LR horizon, and evaluation coordinate:

- scale1.75, seed43122, dense 2M checkpoints.
- scale1.25, seed43022, dense 2M checkpoints.

They test two local statements:

1. whether the scale1.75 late peak near 82M is robust to seed; and
2. whether adapter scale shifts the timing/width of the late competence peak.

They do **not** vary the defining data mechanism behind the proposed context-mediated invariance theory: semantic overlap between paired views and surface-form diversity. Therefore they cannot by themselves establish a transferable learning law.

## Fixed interpretation before reading asset freeze and principles results

Use `scripts/trajectory_falsification_analyzer.py` once trajectory payloads exist. Interpret results as follows.

### Scale1.75 seed43122

Supports seed robustness only if:

- the best cheap7 region is within roughly ±4M words of the seed43022 82M reference;
- there is a real late decline rather than a flat/noisy monotone path;
- the per-column profile is not a single-column artifact.

Weakens seed-robust peak interpretation if:

- the best point is far from the 78M-86M region;
- the high band is too flat to identify a peak;
- the improvement is carried by small-column or capability-specific noise;
- SuperGLUE or later full scoring contradicts cheap7.

### Scale1.25 seed43022

Supports adapter-amplitude-controlled interference only if:

- the near-peak band is later and/or wider than scale1.75 seed43022 when scored on the same cheap7 columns;
- the family profile remains recognizably similar, not a different allocation of BLiMP/COMPS/GlobalPIQA versus EWoK/Entity/Reading;
- the 100M endpoint does not simply underfit at lower scale.

Weakens amplitude-control interpretation if:

- the peak is earlier, scattered, or absent;
- column maxima are far apart in exposure;
- aggregate cheap7 hides strong family-specific tradeoffs;
- the endpoint behaves like a different architecture route rather than a scaled version of the same interference process.

## Orthogonal replication needed for a transferable principle

The high-value general question is whether compact semantic second views plus same-window pairing improve data-efficient learning beyond the specific DeBERTa-v2 MLM coordinate. The cleanest next replication should preserve the matched contrast:

- same legal tokenizer or a legally fitted tokenizer on the same text union;
- same 10M-word budget and <=100M exposure;
- matched corpus streams differing only in semantic-view organization versus exact repetition or independent-breadth text;
- official-compatible zero-shot and, if promising, full scoring;
- a meaningfully different model family or scale.

Preferred division:

- The DeBERTa seed/scale trajectory evaluation and its falsifiable analysis remain in progress.
- An orthogonal architecture/scale comparison is proposed: a GPT-style causal LM or another non-DeBERTa official-compatible model, comparing compact-view reinvest with exact-repeat and/or source-breadth under matched exposure. This would test whether context-mediated invariance appears outside masked bidirectional DeBERTa.

## Minimal reliable architecture-transfer screen

Because a full 3-arm x 100M architecture panel is expensive, the entry test should be the lowest cost that can decide whether the route deserves mature spending:

1. Build or identify an official-compatible non-DeBERTa trainer/evaluator.
2. Train **two matched arms** to an exposure where earlier same-data mechanisms become visible but before endpoint spending dominates, such as 20M or 40M: compact-view-reinvest versus exact-repeat or independent-breadth.
3. Evaluate the same cheap7 columns.
4. Continue to mature exposure only if the compact-view arm shows a broad family-positive movement, not just COMPS/BLiMP/GlobalPIQA redistribution.

A negative architecture-transfer screen does not erase the DeBERTa result; it bounds the generality of the mechanism. A positive screen would materially support the transferable data-efficient learning principle.
