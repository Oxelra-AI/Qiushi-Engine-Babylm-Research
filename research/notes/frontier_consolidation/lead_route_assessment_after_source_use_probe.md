No new H100 training should be launched.  The unresolved evidence has higher decision value:

1. protected-reference common 70M--100M grid: needed to confirm or refute the possible 84M cheap7 peak and to decide whether endpoint work must branch to SuperGLUE evaluation.

## If the pending evidence leaves factorial training as the strongest next experiment

The only currently clean first training screen is:

- compact ordered vs compact scrambled, using the exact same pair lexical multiset and exact-loader-matched exposure, with a short screen first and family-level interpretation across cheap6/cheap5, relation-state, syntax/COMPS, and volatile columns.

A second wave could compare sourcewide_onegap ordered vs sourcewide_onegap scrambled to ask whether ordered source-wide recurrence has the same downstream effect.  Cross-family compact vs sourcewide_onegap or sourcewide_onegap vs prefix_fluent should not be used to claim isolated tail coverage, budget efficiency, or semantic transformation without a new construction that also matches copy-zone composition, active/WWM mass, source-document count, and target lexical distribution.
