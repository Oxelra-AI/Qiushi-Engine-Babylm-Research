# bridge dose response interpretation — accumulated mechanism evidence synthesis

## The scientific object

Natural compact semantic views of FineWeb source texts, generated by Qwen3.5-9B, paired with their sources and interleaved with broader filler from six official BabyLM corpora, trained with whole-word masked language modeling on DeBERTa-v2 (8×480, 34.5M params) under the legal 10M word / 100M word-exposure constraint.

The validated data-level result: compact-reinvest minus clean controls at 80M gives +1.35 mean7, including BLiMP +1.53, Supplement +2.06, EWoK +2.03, Entity +2.33, COMPS +0.65, Reading +0.85 [clean control trained and representation frontier].

## What has been ruled out as the mechanism

### Source/tail access alone (extractive selected readout result)
Source-only extractive views with broad source coverage (extractive_wide: 98.2% content coverage, 100% tail coverage, 0.928 source span) and zero novel content perform WORSE than compact on stable families (cheap6 -0.662, EWoK+Entity -2.820). Missing source/tail access is not the explanation.

### Architecture-general transfer (causal transfer result synthesis, 221)
The compact benefit does not transfer broadly to:
- GPT2 causal LM (cheap6 -0.145)
- Stock RoBERTa absolute-position MLM (cheap6 -0.115 at 100M)
Both show local source-absent NLL advantages but negative or neutral stable-family performance.

### Compact-specific ordered source retrieval (source conditioned ordering interaction synthesis)
Source-conditioned ordering interaction is a general property of bidirectional MLM, not unique to compact. The interaction was smallest for compact (2.748 vs prefix 3.465, sourcewide 3.595).

### Same-sequence source-absent/copied-target complementarity (cross realization probe resolution)
Cross-realization probe found full-minus-drop_abs +0.013 with intervals crossing zero. Target-cooccurrence is not the dominant mechanism.

### Coherent word order at fixed compact multiset (compact order result and roberta transfer decision)
Ordered vs scrambled compact words: by 40M, ordered creates a selective source-absent NLL channel but has WORSE stable-family performance (cheap7 -0.67). Order at fixed words is not sufficient.

### Source-dependent innovation masking (route reopen conditional innovation)
Conditional-innovation masking failed broad transfer (-1.05 cheap7 at 80M). Direct source-absent target prioritization doesn't help.

### True-source correspondence as mature source-free transfer (static decoy contingency validation)
Frozen-82M aligned-tail lost 3.67 Supplement points. Edit-state decodability exists but doesn't survive source removal.

## What remains as the surviving mechanism object

A coupled natural data system:
1. **Fluent contextual re-expression**: compact views are grammatical sentences, not telegraphic word lists
2. **Source-absent generated content**: ~17.3% of content mass (17,891 words) are novel vocabulary from compression
3. **Source-wide lexical coverage via compression**: compact covers 65.7% of source content positions vs repeat's 59.8%, reaching 70.2% tail coverage
4. **Content density**: compact at 0.647 content fraction, close to extractive_balanced (0.632), far from wide (0.811)
5. **Token geometry**: compact generates 247,878 active BPE tokens vs balanced's 238,050 (~4% more, but <0.1% at stream level)
6. **Reinvested source diversity**: saved word budget goes to broader filler exposure

These factors remain entangled. The extractive experiment showed that (3), (4), and (6) alone are insufficient. The bridge experiment (running) tests whether adding adjacency structure to (3)+(4)+(6) closes the gap, or whether (1)+(2) are jointly essential.

## Toward a transferable principle

The accumulated evidence suggests: **faithful semantic compression creates a learning environment where the model must reconstruct meaning from contextually enriched, density-controlled, fluent re-expressions rather than from surface repetition or telegraphic keyword packing.** The downstream benefit is not from any single extracted property (coverage, density, order, novel vocabulary) but from their natural coupling in generated compressed text.

If the bridge experiment confirms that adjacency optimization doesn't close the gap, this supports framing the principle as a **semantic rate-distortion bound**: the compression process naturally produces the right density-coverage-fluency tradeoff that source-only selection cannot achieve because it lacks the generation step that creates fluent re-expression around shared content.
