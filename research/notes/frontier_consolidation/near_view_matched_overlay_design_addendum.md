# Near-Length Second Views on a Matched Overlay

Status: matched construction verified and training initiated; downstream near-view effect was not yet known.

The first behavior comparison paired the same medium-risk factual sources either with local repetition or with near-length generated views. It preserved inherited paired rows and replaced the same 423,520-word official slice. Matching was within this near-length family, not across arbitrary compact and reinvestment variants.

Both 10M pools and ten-pass 100M streams were exact. Inherited paired material contributed 1,656,800 words; 2,647 intact nonpaired rows supplied the 423,520-word replacement budget. Common filler was 9,576,480 words, and the shared near-core construction contained 10,094 sources. Changed rows exceeding 256 model tokens numbered 35 for repetition and 29 for views; mean view-minus-repeat token shift was -0.377 per changed row.

The historical recipe used DeBERTa-v2 8x480 with 8 heads and FFN multiplier 4, fixed WWM 0.15, AdamW learning rate 0.001, warmup 0.06, weight decay 0.01, batch/sequence length 256, seeds 43/43022/43023 and 100,000,000 whitespace-counted words with 1M checkpoint cadence.

The planned readout was near-view minus near-repeat across BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, COMPS, GlobalPIQA and Reading. Broad transfer would motivate compact-core and reinvestment tests; narrow trade-offs required interpretation before further variants. Construction parity was not itself evidence of generated-view value.
