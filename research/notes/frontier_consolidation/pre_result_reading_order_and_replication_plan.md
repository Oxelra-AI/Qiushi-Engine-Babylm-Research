# pre result reading order and replication plan pre-result readout order for the compact-dose program

This note was written before reading any new MAX, midpoint, RoBERTa, or future breadth score rows. It fixes how the incoming numbers should be interpreted to prevent post-hoc selection of a favorable single checkpoint, family, or contrast.

## Research object now in flight

The dose program tests a fixed-word restructuring principle for BabyLM Strict-Small: within a 10M-word budget, replace part of the ordinary clean stream with source-aligned compact semantic second views, then ask whether the resulting experience yields more transferable competence under the fixed legal tokenizer, architecture, MLM recipe, and official-compatible stable-family evaluation.

The constructed vertices are sufficient for the current scientific question:

- DeBERTa 1x, 1.82x, and 2.64x view/repeat ladders: semantic re-expression versus duplicated recurrence under shared source rows.
- Sparse clean reference rows: clean stream reference at the checkpoints already owned, used with caution because it is not a full ten-checkpoint clean ladder.
- RoBERTa 2.64x view/clean: second-architecture total fixed-budget transfer/break condition, not semantic isolation.
- DeBERTa 2.64x breadth arm materialized on CPU: fixed-word allocation of companion budget to additional same-population FineWeb sentences rather than compact rewrites; no H100 training has been submitted for this arm.

No further construction vertices should be added now. RoBERTa repeat, higher-than-2.64x dose, and new-document breadth are not the next work unless the incoming evidence makes one exact second-seed replication necessary.

## Composite hierarchy

Primary composites for dose and transfer statements:

1. `cheap6_no_GlobalPIQA` = BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, COMPS, Reading.
2. `cheap5_no_GlobalPIQA_Reading` = BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, COMPS.

A dose, transfer, or allocation statement needs cheap6 and cheap5 to agree in sign and broad shape. If they disagree, the result is a family reallocation pattern that needs family/item reading, not a principle.

Secondary evidence:

- `EWoK_plus_Entity_sum` is useful for relation/state sensitivity, but it cannot carry a statement alone because earlier analysis measured a much larger seed spread for this sum (about 2.26 over the ladder) than for cheap6 (about 0.11).
- Individual families must be reported with sign structure, especially BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, COMPS, and Reading. GlobalPIQA is not part of the stable readout and must not rescue a result.
- SuperGLUE and AoA are outside the current mechanism readout and should not be used to reinterpret dose effects.

## Reading order for DeBERTa dose ladders

1. Confirm identity and completeness of the new rows before interpreting them: exact arm names, all ten checkpoints for 1x/1.82x/2.64x view and repeat, fixed spatial repair route status tokenizer, full DeBERTa p2c+c2p recipe, exact 100M exposure for trained arms, finite losses, and expected row/update geometry. A partial ladder is not a curve.

2. Read ladder means across all ten checkpoints before reading endpoints. The curve statement rests on the mean exposure profile, not a single favorable 80M or 100M row.

3. For each dose, compute the semantic leg:

   `V-R = compact_view_d - compact_repeat_d`

   This is the cleanest within-dose semantic re-expression contrast because source rows, row lengths, filler, tokenizer, seed triplet, masking recipe, and training geometry are shared within that dose.

4. Read clean-free growth against the owned 1x reference:

   `V_d - V_1 = [(V_d - R_d) - (V_1 - R_1)] + (R_d - R_1)`

   This is the main dose-growth statement because the 1x reference cancels and all ten checkpoints are available. It separates semantic growth from repeat/source growth without relying on sparse clean rows.

5. Read sparse clean contrasts only after the clean-free curve:

   `V-C = (V-R) + (R-C)`

   These rows are useful for total restructuring and source-admission interpretation, but they are sparse in checkpoint support and carry source-population / row-geometry conditions. They cannot override the all-checkpoint clean-free profile.

6. Only after the above, inspect individual families and per-target movement. Family movement explains which competencies are reallocated; it does not replace the primary cheap6/cheap5 shape.

## Reading order for RoBERTa MAX view-clean

RoBERTa 2.64x view-clean is an amplified total fixed-budget transfer test. It should be read as:

- Does the largest DeBERTa total-effect construction transfer to a stock RoBERTa 8x480 MLM coordinate at 2.64x?
- Primary readout: cheap6 and cheap5 across the ten checkpoint ladder.
- Family signs must be shown. A positive result concentrated only in a volatile small family is not enough.

It must not be described as semantic isolation. The view-clean contrast includes source admission, freed-budget breadth, row order, sequence and masking geometry, tokenizer interaction, and row/update effects.

If RoBERTa view-clean is positive but the DeBERTa semantic leg is not the carrier, do not add RoBERTa repeat just to decompose a small term. If RoBERTa view-clean is null or negative at amplified dose, the architecture condition is sharper than the earlier 1x RoBERTa/GPT2 view-repeat nulls.

## Reading order for MAX view-breadth if it is later trained

The breadth arm is not a new-document expansion and not semantic isolation. It is a fixed-word allocation contrast:

- Same MAX source sentences are preserved: 33,291 sources / 690,465 source words.
- The 428,122 compact rewrite words are replaced by 15,123 independent whole FineWeb sentences totaling exactly 428,122 words.
- The selected breadth sentences are largely from the same document population as MAX sources: selected document overlap fraction 0.9491.

pre result reading order and replication plan CPU measurements to carry with any view-breadth score:

- Complete 10M pools are exactly word-matched at 10,000,000 words and row-matched at 65,313 rows.
- Whole-pool relative shifts versus MAX view: repeat has -0.4021% legal16k tokens and +0.0056% WWM groups; clean has +0.4095% legal16k tokens and -0.0638% WWM groups; breadth has -0.2864% legal16k tokens and +0.0086% WWM groups.
- Changed block only: breadth has -2.6015% legal16k tokens and -2.5155% visible tokens versus view, but +0.0753% visible WWM groups. Repeat has -3.6522% legal16k tokens versus view but +0.0492% WWM groups.
- Rowwise WWM group counts are almost identical (breadth vs view exact count fraction 0.9797), but visible word-start patterns are never identical at the row level; normalized pattern distance is 0.3746 for breadth vs view and 0.3348 for repeat vs view.
- Compact rewrites have higher token fertility than breadth sentences: 1.52865 tokens/word for rewrites versus 1.43079 for selected breadth and 1.38452 for MAX sources.
- Replaced companion unigram distribution differs: rewrite-vs-breadth JS divergence 0.1242 bits. Symmetric coarse text-domain JS is 0.0409 bits.
- Breadth selection is length-biased by construction: selected breadth mean 28.31 words versus candidate mean 20.96.
- Breadth rows have fewer sentence-final marks than view rows: -2.033 per changed row. Boundary geometry differs.

Thus a future view-breadth score tests whether source-conditioned compact re-expression beats extra same-population sentence experience under matched words, matched row counts, and nearly matched WWM group opportunity, while also carrying small token, subword-pattern, lexical-population, sentence-boundary, and length-selection conditions.

## Replication rule after first ladders

Prior experiments measured that single-run few-tenth effects can be basin-specific. Therefore the next experiment should not create a fifth vertex. It should repeat the exact MAX-dose leg that carries the effect, with an independent seed/basin and the same fixed tokenizer, recipe, row geometry, and exposure.

Choose the second-seed MAX contrast only after the first ladders are complete:

- If semantic growth carries the dose effect and cheap6/cheap5 agree, replicate `MAX view-repeat`: train both 2.64x view and 2.64x repeat under the independent seed setting.
- If total restructuring carries the effect while semantic/source split is mixed, replicate `MAX view-clean`: train both 2.64x view and the matched clean arm under the independent seed setting.
- If source-admission/repeat growth carries the effect and cheap6/cheap5 agree, replicate `MAX repeat-clean`: train both 2.64x repeat and the matched clean arm under the independent seed setting.
- If the future trained breadth arm carries the fixed-word allocation result, replicate `MAX view-breadth`: train both 2.64x view and 2.64x breadth under the independent seed setting.
- If cheap6 and cheap5 do not agree, or the ladder-mean profile is small relative to the earlier analysis seed spread, do not spend a new GPU run on replication; instead read family/item movement and update the mechanism.

This rule makes a second-basin effect evidence for a data-efficient learning principle. A single-basin favorable leg remains only one draw from the same stochastic competence-allocation process already exposed in earlier analysis.
