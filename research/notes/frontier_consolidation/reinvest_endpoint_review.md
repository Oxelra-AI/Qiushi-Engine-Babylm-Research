# reinvest endpoint review review of the compact_view_reinvest 42.0868 endpoint

CPU-only critical review of the completed `compact_view_reinvest` seed43022 endpoint. It is treated as a provisional strongest complete result, not a finished final claim. AoA localization and compact-repeat SuperGLUE evaluation remain pending; no new training or evaluation was launched for this review.

## 1. Score and official arithmetic: CONFIRMED

- Nine official columns present: BLiMP 66.87, Supplement 63.28, EWoK 53.67, Entity 27.75,
  COMPS 51.97, SuperGLUE 71.3811, GlobalPIQA 35.62, Reading 8.24, AoA 0.0.
- Independent recomputation of Overall = mean(9 columns) = **42.0867857**, exactly matching
  the recorded value (delta 0.0). NLP_average 52.9344, Human_like 4.12 also reproduce.
- The local scoring helper `experiments/archive/compact_experience/scripts/babylm_official_scoring.py`
  matches the official leaderboard convention: its `leaderboard_overall` self-test reproduces
  a real leaderboard row's Overall 40.62 with AoA scaled by 100. Overall is the mean of the
  nine leaderboard-unit columns. This is the same aggregation the visible 41.8 leader uses.
- Delta vs COMPACT_EXPERIENCE clean-Qwen: **+0.7425**. Delta vs visible 41.8 leader: **+0.2868**.

## 2. AoA=0 is legitimate but is the load-bearing fragility

- Official gate (`evaluation_pipeline/utils.py`): if the model-vs-child AoA Pearson p>0.1,
  `curve_fitness` is set to 0.0. Reinvest has raw correlation 0.0 (curve_fitness 0.0, n_words
  203), so AoA leaderboard score = 0.0. This is rule-correct and identical in convention to
  clean-Qwen and the leader (both 0.0).
- Sensitivity (independent computation, all other columns fixed):
  - AoA raw 0.00 -> Overall 42.0868 (current)
  - AoA raw -0.05 -> Overall 41.5312 (below leader)
  - AoA raw -0.10 -> Overall 40.9757
  - AoA raw -0.1269 (compact_core's value) -> Overall 40.6768 (below clean-Qwen)
- Interpretation: the entire +0.2868 lead over the leader survives only while AoA stays at
  the non-significant gate. compact_core already showed that a small raw-correlation shift
  (-0.1109 clean -> -0.1269) can cross p<=0.1 and cost ~12.7 leaderboard points. Therefore
  the second seed (seed43122) is genuinely decisive, not a formality: if the AoA correlation
  is seed-sensitive near the significance boundary, the lead is not yet robust.

## 4. Eval-contamination safeguard: PASSES, with a residual manual-inspection caveat

- The changed FineWeb block's exact 7-gram overlap with official eval text is LOWER than the
  heldout official rows it replaced: score-bearing rows -12, GLUE-valid rows -14, unique
  7-grams -103. Net, the swap reduced measured phrase overlap.
- The sampled overlaps are generic public phrases ("at the height of the cold war",
  "in many other parts of the world") and land overwhelmingly on GLUE *train* splits used for
  fine-tuning, not held-out zero-shot targets. This is a provenance warning, not evidence of
  test contamination.
- Residual caveat: exact 7-gram matching misses paraphrase-level overlap, and a small number
  of GLUE-*valid* hits exist. For a submission-grade contamination statement, the few
  glue_valid_scored hits (7 by file role) should be directly inspected, and ideally a
  near-duplicate (not just exact) measure run. This is a verification step, not a blocker to
  treating 42.0868 as the current best result.

## 5. Scientific substance: real but partly entangled

- Reinvest is not the failed compact_core (Overall 39.99). Reinvest holds AoA at 0.0 and
  carries the compact-density gains: vs clean-Qwen +3.48 EWoK, +1.99 Entity, +1.07 SuperGLUE,
  +0.48 Reading, +0.44 Supplement, +0.19 COMPS, +0.03 BLiMP, only -1.00 GlobalPIQA.
- The mechanism is best stated as: redundancy-reduced generated semantic second views plus
  reinvested source diversity under a fixed 10M-word budget. It is NOT compaction alone
  (compaction alone failed) and NOT source substitution alone.
- Entanglement remains: compact-view transformation, denoising, lexical-token geometry, and
  added source breadth co-vary. The pending AoA localization and
  compact_repeat SuperGLUE attribution are the cheapest way to separate them.

## Review verdict

- The 42.0868 endpoint is a genuine, rule-compliant, provenance-clean single-run result that
  clears the visible 41.8 leader by +0.2868 under the correct official arithmetic. This is the
  study's strongest complete result and the new active reference.
- It is NOT yet a finished final SOTA claim, for two concrete reasons:
  1. The +0.2868 margin is small and rests entirely on the AoA non-significance gate; a single
     seed's AoA landing significant would erase the lead. seed43122 (running) is decisive.
  2. Submission-grade contamination requires direct inspection of the few glue_valid overlap
     rows (and preferably a near-duplicate check) plus confirmation of the exact server-side
     aggregation from the current babylm-eval repo/leaderboard, which uses the same 9-column
     mean reproduced here.
- No new 100M training is warranted to establish the result. The remaining work is reading
  pending experimental evidence (AoA localization, compact_repeat SuperGLUE, seed43122) and
  finishing the contamination inspection, then a packaging/submission decision.
