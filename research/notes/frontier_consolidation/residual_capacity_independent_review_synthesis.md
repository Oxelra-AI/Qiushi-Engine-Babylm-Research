## New constructions to prefer if fixed scale1.75 is mixed but not enough

- Norm-budgeted per-layer residual amplitude: zero-output adapters with learned or fixed per-layer gates but a benchmark-independent functional branch-energy cap, e.g. penalize RMS update ratio above a fixed small rho. Tests whether branch energy causes mature world/reading erosion.
- Depth-local residual capacity with matched parameter count: lower-only, upper-only, middle/upper, alternating. Tests whether state-tracking gains can be localized away from broad semantic/reading channels.
- Fixed-write scratch space: adapter writes only into a fixed seed-generated orthonormal subspace while reading all hidden features. Tests whether stable coordinates can hold entity-state variables without rotating shared semantic channels.
- Slow residual basis / fast zero-initialized readout: fix or slow the adapter down-projection while training the zero-initialized up projection normally. Tests whether moving adapter bases plus moving backbone drive nonmonotone tradeoffs.
- Forward-output orthogonal residual (distinct from Muon): project adapter contribution away from current hidden stream or penalize alignment. Tests whether complementary residual features, rather than amplification of existing features, carry the gains.
