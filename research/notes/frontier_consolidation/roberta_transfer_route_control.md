## Current evidence state

The active research object is the natural compact-second-view marginal: compact generated views plus reinvested source diversity, compared against first-N repeat controls under the same legal word budget. The strongest validated effect remains in the legal DeBERTa MLM coordinate; the GPT2 causal coordinate failed to show broad transfer; the compact ordered/scrambled contrast showed that a source-absent local NLL channel can emerge while stable official-compatible families worsen.

## Interpretation After RoBERTa Evaluation

Use official-compatible selected movement, not training loss or local pseudolikelihood alone, to decide the next expensive step.

If RoBERTa compact-minus-repeat is late-positive on stable selected families — especially cheap6 without GlobalPIQA, cheap5 without GlobalPIQA/Reading, EWoK+Entity, Supplement, Entity, and COMPS — and the item/subtask movement agrees with the local source-absent relational/event channel, then the only admissible expensive continuation is a paired independent-seed RoBERTa compact-vs-repeat robustness run, with the smallest checkpoint/evaluation schedule sufficient to test recurrence.

If selected movement is neutral, negative, or carried by GlobalPIQA/Reading while the local semantic channel remains positive, stock RoBERTa becomes another local-versus-downstream split. Then do not replicate RoBERTa and do not re-open explicit source-absent target weighting; redirect to natural compact-data decomposition: content density, source-wide coverage, lexical recurrence, faithful compression, and diversity reinvestment.

If selected transfer is stable but the local source-absent relational/event structure does not align, treat transfer as real for this coordinate while keeping mechanism formation open; inspect item/subtask movement before choosing at most one clean natural data intervention.
