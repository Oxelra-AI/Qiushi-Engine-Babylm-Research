# Seed-Noise Ladders and Reinterpretation of Small Effects

Status: proposed and initiated measurement program; no completed second-seed ladder was supplied by this record.

Small single-seed differences of a few tenths had driven several mechanism decisions. Binary item-delta decorrelation raised a measurement question: how large was compact-minus-repeat seed spread under the same full-DeBERTa learning coordinate? The intended quantity was an exposure-profile distribution and seed-spread distribution, not a selected endpoint.

The design compared compact and repetition at seeds 43022 and 43122, retaining tokenizer, streams, full c2p+p2c model, WWM 0.15, AdamW, batch/sequence length 256, 10M checkpoint cadence and 100M exposure. The stable-family ladder covered BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, Entity, COMPS and Reading at every 10M boundary.

The readout asked whether treatment-profile signs and peaks recurred across seeds, whether cheap6/cheap5/EWoK-plus-Entity gains exceeded same-coordinate spread, and whether earlier architecture/data differences were large relative to that spread. Reproducible family movement would support a systematic learning component; comparable or opposing seed movement would require describing small effects as seed-dependent redistribution rather than stable acquisition.

A separate EWoK margin probe specified 10 rows per domain at 80M and 100M across all four cells. Correlated continuous treatment deltas despite uncorrelated binary flips would favor margin allocation; decorrelated margins would strengthen the seed-dependent interpretation. Earlier mechanically matched results were not erased by this calibration, but their resolution and generality would change. No outcome of this proposed calibration should be inferred from the existence of its analysis code.
