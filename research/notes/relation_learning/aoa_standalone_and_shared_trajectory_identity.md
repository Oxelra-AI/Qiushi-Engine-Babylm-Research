# Standalone and Shared AoA Trajectories

Scientific status: final provenance clarification of existing measurements.

Two genuine AoA measurements coexist. The repaired main comparison at Overall 42.024 used the shared 18-point trajectory. The standalone first-generation measurement and its public prediction package used a 19-checkpoint trajectory. Their respective finite-row counts were 144,090 and 152,095. Both received the evaluator score 0.0, so this distinction did not change that Overall coordinate.

The 19-checkpoint trajectory is not a placeholder or an unrelated auxiliary analysis. Conversely, its presence in the standalone prediction package does not prove that the main repaired comparison used it. A trajectory's estimator input, model ancestry, endpoint identity, and package provenance must be recorded separately.

This clarification supersedes the interim shorthand that assigned a single anatomy to all first-generation measurements or described the 19-checkpoint run merely as a separate detailed analysis. It does not retroactively alter either raw trajectory or score.

The 30M timing experiment is another distinct object: it compares row-order and masking-credit interventions at one seed. Its correlations and fitted-word populations cannot replace either endpoint trajectory in the main comparison.
