## Corrections from earlier analysis and corrected composition and trust

### 1. Composition (HM/HV) section corrected

The corrected two component frame "cooperation" interpretation was wrong. HM−HV compact nonoverlap ΔA_T = +0.33
is driven entirely by neutral/unrelated anchor movement (ΔN = +0.37, ΔU = +0.14) while
true-source NLL is essentially unchanged (HM T=6.276, HV T=6.233, HV slightly better).

**HV is not a clean dose control**: its 16,560 source+neutral-filling pairs actively install
prediction of compact targets after non-corresponding prefixes. HV has the lowest N (7.78)
and U (7.62) of all 5 arms despite having the least compact exposure. This deflates HV's A_T.

**Wikipedia tells a different story**: HM−HV overlap Δ(N−T) = +0.292 (SE 0.047) with
Δ(N−U) = +0.005 — the entire difference IS true-source use, no anchor movement.
At equal rewrite dose, replacing neutral filling with exact copies takes natural source-recurring
use from CLEAN level to full VIEW level.

**The one confident claim**: exact-recurrence liability on natural substitutions saturates at
half dose and is not offset by co-practiced restatement.
- HM−C nonoverlap Δ(N−T) = −0.214 vs R−C = −0.207
- Half the exact dose carries REPEAT's full natural substitution liability
- The 16.7K co-practiced rewrites do not offset any of it

Neither "cooperation" nor "additive" describes both tables correctly.
