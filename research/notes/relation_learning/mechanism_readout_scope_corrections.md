# Mechanism Readout Scope Corrections

Scientific status: retrospective corrections to the interpretation and display of existing evidence.

A component ladder must retain its actual baseline. Absolute BLiMP, Supplement, EWoK, and COMPS values cannot be displayed on a narrow axis intended for Entity-scale values without making the truncation explicit. Relative-to-matched-ordinary deltas expose acquisition costs and partial recovery more faithfully. Historical stripped-loader Overall 42.1210 is a separate reference, not another repaired-coordinate bar.

Temperature-refitted source specificity and rank-based source specificity are measured readouts. They are not respectively a prediction panel and an outcome panel. Their sign conventions, seeds, target counts, and source conditions must be explicit. The prospective prediction belongs in the experimental rationale, distinct from the numerical measurement.

Dense-common positions are not acquisition label positions. The common-support probe measures a fixed intersection of ordinary-mask targets and dense nonlabel masked positions. A title claiming that this probe directly measures erasure at the label positions overstates its scope. Dense-state KL and ordinary-rendering preservation KL are also different quantities.

The dense-preservation source readout did not include an Entity score for that control. Its behavioral prediction cannot be promoted to a measured benchmark result. The tested coefficient was lambda=1 at one training seed; equal coefficients do not establish equal constraint strength.

In the 30M AoA comparison, the arm-specific fitted-word counts were 282, 157, and 271. Correlations based on different fitted vocabularies are not identical-sample contrasts. The common-word shift correlation is a third statistical object, distinct from either arm's model-child correlation. Definitions and sample sizes are more informative than unexplained significance symbols.
