# From Relation Studies to Preservation: Route Revisions

Scientific status: historical synthesis. Failed routes and narrowed claims are retained; proposals are not promoted to results.

The initial scalar-value interpretation became untenable when aggregate effects changed with seed while particular Entity contrasts remained reproducible. Row-level intervention effects also had low cross-seed reproducibility: the recorded raw/residualized correlations were 0.0247/0.0567. The scientific question shifted from which text is globally better to which relation-dependent prediction behavior is learned.

Local exact recurrence and nonidentical restatement had opposite true-source effects; unrelated-source controls and source/companion splitting tested their specificity and locality. This supports a relation-typed interpretation for the tested windows, not a universal requirement across all language learning. Natural target-form transfer further separated narrow restatement competence from more broadly expressed recurrence costs.

## Failed or Restricted Routes

1. State-update replacement excluded several copying and temporal cues but did not produce stable two-seed Entity improvement. The intended operation could still be bypassed by recency, position, or format.
2. Descriptor binding initially looked like discrete state assignment. The interpretation was revised to mention-conditioned summary readout, particularly when the descriptor was not actually present in context.
3. Literal-value binding improved the local assignment task but transferred poorly to Entity. Source retention and changed-state tracking are distinct behaviors; successful local assignment is not sufficient evidence of update competence.
4. Additional aligned-relation dose on an already aligned base produced limited marginal gains. The available discrete arms do not by themselves fit a universal saturation law.
5. Coherent-row preservation did not automatically control short-row or special-token behavior. Constraints apply to the represented combination of input format, masking state, and loss positions.

## Prospective State-Geometry Test

Before the dense-preservation control was trained, the recorded prediction was that matching the starting model on the dense, evidence-poor acquisition state would suppress source-responsive acquisition. The later control used the same Qwen rows, direct 86M starting teacher, nominal lambda=1, seed62064, and 80 optimizer updates.

The recorded source-specificity/rank changes were ordinary +0.000/-1.2, acquisition +0.147/+67.0, ordinary-state preservation +0.101/+42.7, and dense-state preservation +0.004/+1.6. This supports the predicted direction on that readout. Entity was not measured for the dense-preservation control. Equal nominal lambda does not match effective constraint strength, and one coefficient/seed does not identify a unique geometry mechanism.

The later interpretation also withdrew exclusive-source and pure-teacher conclusions. Acquisition suppresses competing local cues; ordinary-state preservation retains much of the acquired response while limiting drift. Rollback and the absent no-teacher extra-presentation control leave the preservation residual only partially decomposed.
