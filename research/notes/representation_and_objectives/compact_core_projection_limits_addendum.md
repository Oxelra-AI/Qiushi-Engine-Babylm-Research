# Compact-Core Projection and Replication Limits

Status: historical fast-screen evidence and conditional projection, not a completed official Overall result.

At seed 43022, compact-view core scored 43.9279 on equal7 with full Entity, versus 41.7664 for matched repetition, a +2.1614 difference. The summed difference across EWoK, Entity, COMPS and mean GlobalPIQA was +7.2200.

Reaching an Overall of 41.8 would require SuperGLUE plus AoA of at least 69.255. Substituting the clean-Qwen reference sum of 70.309 gives a scenario Overall of 41.9171, conditional on the fast surface surviving full evaluation. This is not a measured compact-model Overall.

A second-seed view-only run tests endpoint robustness, not replication of the treatment effect. That requires a matched second-seed repetition arm. The existing contrast supported generated compact views over exact repetition at one seed; it did not yet establish seed-stable effects or compression isolated from rewriting, order, truncation and semantic alteration.
