## Positive facts that survived

1. **Compact faithful semantic views are real in the successful masked-denoising coordinate.** In the matched DeBERTa-v2 MLM triangle, `compact_view_reinvest` scored 44.2886 equal7 versus 41.8721 for literal `compact_repeat_reinvest`; view-repeat +2.4164 exceeds the predeclared fast equal7 seed-spread band 1.3557. This is broad plus targeted, not a one-column spike.

2. **The adjacency-broken rewrite marginal is not a collapse control.** `adjbreak_reinvest` scored 43.0557 equal7, retaining strong broad competence and beating repeat by +1.1836 even though it had much worse local MLM loss. Therefore local predictability/final training loss cannot explain downstream competence.

3. **Correctness transitions reveal opposing effects.** correctness transition analysis showed adjbreak-repeat gains grammar (+209 BLiMP net) but loses EWoK (-27), while view-adjbreak gains Supplement/EWoK relational competence (+9 Supplement, +51 EWoK in relational domains) but loses BLiMP (-217). The aggregate view-adjbreak gap (+1.2329) is inside seed band because grammar and relational effects cancel, not because the arms are similar.

4. **Frozen source-conditioned ordering is large but not compact-specific.** hypothesis comparison found large DeBERTa source-conditioned ordering interactions for compact, prefix, and onegap; compact has the smallest per-target interaction because many rewrite targets are source-absent. This supports a more general ordered-source visibility/retrieval mechanism plus tail coverage and budget efficiency, rather than a unique compact semantic recoding mechanism.

5. **The causal-GPT route did not transfer broadly.** The original causal compact-vs-repeat transfer leg was broad-null/negative beyond GlobalPIQA, and compact directional internal result/216's forked FF/RR/FR/RF compact causal topology learned directional side prediction but failed to produce robust broad or distributed relational reciprocal synergy. Full EWoK interaction was negative; the correctness transition analysis relational-domain average was not domain-distributed and the bootstrap interval included zero. Do not launch semantic_extract/random_extract causal controls from that premise.

## Current mechanism object

The surviving object is **masked-denoising cross-view visibility under budget pressure**:

- A compact side view increases content density and tail coverage under the same 10M-word pool.
- Bidirectional MLM attention lets a masked token in either source or side view condition on nearby ordered source evidence.
- Copied tokens receive strong direct retrieval benefit; noncopied compact rewrite tokens are weaker locally but may force abstraction, paraphrase normalization, or relation-preserving neighborhood formation.
- Downstream gains arise only when this visibility/coverage changes reusable competence rather than becoming a local reconstruction shortcut.

This object is more precise than “reciprocal causal exchange” and remains inside the successful DeBERTa MLM geometry, but it must predict transfer beyond the exact current coordinate. A useful next intervention should therefore manipulate **cross-view visibility and copied-versus-noncopied target coverage** in the MLM trainer while preserving the same aligned source/side multiset and legal word budget.

## Proposed Intervention Family

A promising next direction is an MLM-native factorial design, not another causal packet variation:

- Use the existing compact source--rewrite pairs and possibly matched source-only extractive views.
- Keep total word exposure, tokenizer, architecture, optimizer, WWM rate, and source/side word multiset matched.
- Vary whether source and side tokens are visible in the same attention window during MLM prediction:
  - intact adjacent windows where masks can attend across source--side boundary;
  - same side-view marginals but boundary-blocked attention or separated chunks, preserving local text but removing cross-view conditioning;
  - copied-target-heavy source extracts with matched tail coverage versus compact rewrites, to separate ordered retrieval from noncopied semantic abstraction;
  - schedule variants where early epochs maximize cross-view visibility and late epochs restore marginal syntactic diversity, because correctness transition analysis suggests relational and grammar components may trade off.
- Readout should not rely only on equal7: measure masked-token losses by copied/noncopied zone, correctness transition analysis relational EWoK domains, Supplement retained-plus-new transitions, BLiMP losses, and whether noncopied targets carry gains not reproduced by copied source extracts.

The key transfer prediction is: if the principle is objective-conditioned cross-view visibility rather than DeBERTa accident, then any bidirectional or denoising model with explicit within-window cross-view attention and matched tail coverage should show the relational/Supplement component, while unidirectional or boundary-blocked conditions should retain only marginal density/grammar effects. If source-only extractive views with equal tail coverage reproduce most gains, the principle is ordered retrieval plus coverage; if compact noncopied targets uniquely move relational/Supplement transitions, the principle includes semantic abstraction through faithful compression.

## adjusted source use residual update: copied-token retrieval is not the compact channel

A cheap adjusted analysis of frozen chck82 source-use records (note 218) sharpened point 4. After matching copy opportunity on copied targets, compact's source-conditioned ordering interaction is significantly *lower* than the fluent/onegap extractive families (compact − mean extractives median −0.491 nats, 95% [−0.605, −0.384], p>0=0.000). Compact's raw I_f deficit is composition (0.217 source-absent fraction vs 0.005 for extractives; source-absent I_f only 0.908). This is the strongest cheap evidence against "compact wins by stronger ordered copied-token retrieval" and shifts weight toward source-absent semantic supervision. The next intervention must therefore separate copied vs source-absent target losses and use copied-only extractive arms as the null; do not assume copied retrieval is the load-bearing channel.
