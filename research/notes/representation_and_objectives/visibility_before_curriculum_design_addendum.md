# Visibility Recovery Before Context Curriculum

Status: prepared design only; no training or evaluation occurred in this record.

The first proposed experience-utilization arm was faithful fixed-length U256 chunking. A short-to-long U64/U128/U256 comparison would follow only after U256, because it adds context-length, order and packing changes to recovery of previously invisible suffixes.

Nominal WWM probability 0.15 defined the initial practical comparison, including ordinary prediction pressure on newly visible suffix tokens. Target-matched probabilities 0.14745584 and 0.14752237 were prepared for later attribution, not silently substituted into the main contrast.

Architecture choice depended on the independent depth result. A strong depth result warranted reproduction; competitive but incomplete depth improvement motivated 12x384 U256; a flat or adverse depth result motivated 8x480 U256 against its existing row-256 baseline. This ordering was intended to distinguish visibility recovery from curriculum and architecture interactions, not to treat a working chunking implementation as evidence of sequence efficacy.
