# earlier analysis — Route decision after the negative matched MNTP 10M result

## What the mntp hybrid s1 10m coordinate evidence establishes (and does not)

Matched 10M MNTP-vs-S1 (same data/order/exposure/updates/schedule/init; only every 16th batch is causal MNTP, 15 MNTP + 230 WWM steps, 133,272 MNTP predicted tokens ≈ 6.15%):

| column | MNTP 10M | S1 10M | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BLiMP | 50.78 | 53.37 | -2.59 |
| Supplement | 54.19 | 52.34 | +1.85 |
| EWoK | 50.48 | 50.60 | -0.12 |
| Entity | 17.39 | 17.73 | -0.34 |
| COMPS | 49.73 | 50.34 | -0.61 |
| GlobalPIQA mean | 34.75 | 35.21 | -0.46 |
| Reading mean | 7.40 | 8.35 | -0.95 |

**Falsified:** the specific claim that this exact fixed every-16 deterministic MNTP form, at early 10M scale, improves the target-gap cluster (Entity/EWoK/COMPS/GlobalPIQA). It does not; it only lifts Supplement while damaging BLiMP and Reading.

**Not falsified:** (a) the relative MNTP-vs-WWM effect at 100M (only ~245/2442 of the schedule was run); (b) other hybrid forms (different ratio, random vs periodic assignment, late-stage introduction, continuous blocks, distribution distillation across views); (c) MNTP in other architectures/tokenizers.

**Two verifier cautions to honor:**
- Do NOT compare 10M MNTP to the public leader's mature coordinate to judge MNTP; the only causal quantity is MNTP−WWM at matched budget.
- Do NOT chase Supplement-only gains: Supplement 54.19 is still below leader 56.04, and the column trade is net-negative without SuperGLUE/AoA.

## Confirmed BabyLM Strict-Small rules relevant to the data route (2026 CfP, `\cite{choshen2026babylmf}`)

- Custom data mixtures are allowed; participants need not use the official corpus.
- ≤10M words, ≤10 epochs, intermediate checkpoints required.
- **All training text counts toward the 10M budget, including text generated by external models.** Synthetic/generated data is not "free."
- External models must come from a predetermined BabyLM list; distillation (revealing an external model's weights/hidden states/output distribution/tokenizer) is prohibited.
- Consequence: a legal binding-rich data route must either (i) use openly licensed existing text within the 10M budget, or (ii) generate synthetic text with an allowed external model but still count every generated word against the 10M budget.

## Legally accessible binding/relation/state resources found (source-verified representations)

- **OpenPI2.0** (`\cite{zhang2023openpia}`): MIT license, entity tracking in procedural text, before/after state changes. Directly relevant to Entity Tracking mechanism.
- **ProPara** (`\cite{mishra2018tracking}`): AllenAI procedural state-change dataset (existence/location), 488 paragraphs, 81k annotations.
- **CLUTRR** (`\cite{sinha2019clutrr}`): semi-synthetic kinship relation stories with templated + paraphrased natural language; inductive relation reasoning.
- **TF1-EN-3M** (`\cite{nadas2025three}`): 3M synthetic English fables (character/trait/setting/conflict/resolution/moral), HF `klusai/ds-tf1-en-3m`; entity+event+state structure in narrative form.
- **WebNLG++ / TailNLG / Spanish WebNLG**: RDF-triple→text verbalizations (CC-BY / GPL); factual relation propositions but small and often multilingual.
- **WikiSnap25** (CC0): structured Wikipedia/Wikidata snapshot; NOT natural-language verbalization — low direct value as pretraining text.
- **EWoK repository** (`\cite{ewokcore2024ewok}`): evaluation set; TERMS_OF_USE forbid training use without acknowledgment and prohibit plaintext distribution; contains canary strings. **Must not be used as training data.**

Mechanistic support that data statistics shape entity capability:
- **Pretraining data statistics shape phases of learning entity comparison** (`\cite{chan2026pretraining}`): entity frequency distribution (Zipfian→uniform) controls whether models move past frequency/position heuristics to correct-answer selection. Strong support that entity/relation data composition — not just density within the official corpus — is a real lever.
- **(How) Do Language Models Track State?** (`\cite{li2025howa}`) and **Do LMs Track Entities Across State Changes?** (`\cite{tang2026language}`): state tracking is learnable and can be induced by intermediate training tasks; models aggregate state non-incrementally. Supports designing explicit state/relation supervision.

## Route decision

Two orthogonal, high-value execution lines. Both must share S1 contract (data/order/init/updates/schedule/eval coordinate) except the single manipulated variable, and both must report internal-mechanism metrics before official columns, plus at least matched-seed replication.

### Route P (primary): proposition-coverage + representation-granularity data experiment
Replace (not add) 15–20% of the lowest-proposition-density official text with legally licensed, proposition-dense entity/relation/state text (OpenPI2.0, ProPara, CLUTRR, and optionally verbalized WebNLG), keeping total words = 10M. This tests the hypothesized missing variable: proposition inventory, which official-corpus reselection/structure-density/passive-rewrite never changed. Because `\cite{chan2026pretraining}` shows entity-frequency distribution matters, control and record entity/relation frequency statistics.
- First decisive experiment: a matched pair {official 10M} vs {official + 15–20% licensed proposition-dense replacement, 10M}, S1 shape/tokenizer/schedule, seed 42 (+ replication seed).
- Optional 2×2 with tokenizer {16k, 40k} only if the replacement main effect is promising, since 40k changes representation granularity (and embedding params — must parameter-match or report).
- Record: entity/relation/state proposition coverage added; entity frequency distribution; joint change on Entity/EWoK/COMPS/GlobalPIQA; guard on BLiMP/Supplement/Reading.
- Compliance: verify every added word counts; keep total ≤10M; do NOT use EWoK materials; if synthetic generation is used later, it must use an allowed external model and count words.

### Route H (secondary): understand the MNTP learning curve rather than blind 100M
Do NOT scale the exact every-16 MNTP to 100M on faith. Instead run the mid-curve matched extension (20M, 40M) of WWM vs every-16 MNTP with the 10M point as curve start, estimating the slope of MNTP−WWM per target column. Add a cheap token-level objective-identity test (first/last token, padding, adjacent masks, multi-subword whole-word masks) before trusting the MNTP label-shift. This decides whether any hybrid-objective variant deserves further compute, or whether the objective route is closed in this implementation.

## What NOT to do
- No 100M MNTP scale-up without a positive mid-curve slope on a target column.
- No Supplement-only optimization.
- No use of EWoK data or any distillation from external models.
- No treating synthetic/generated text as outside the 10M budget.
- No further official-corpus-only reselection/structure-density/passive-rewrite variants (closed).

## Recommended next execution
Start Route P: it directly attacks the target-gap cluster with a mechanism (proposition inventory + entity frequency distribution) that the closed routes never manipulated, is legally grounded in verified sources, and is cheap at 10M given BabyLM's fast training. Route H can run in parallel as a lower-cost diagnostic of the objective route.
