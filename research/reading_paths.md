# Curated Reading Paths Through the Original Research

These 32 reading paths are organized by scientific question. They connect the questions, design choices, results, and subsequent corrections recorded at the time, rather than repeating the three-stage overview. Start each path with its note or plan, then follow the evidence links to inspect the actual comparison. "Next steps" and "in progress" in an original note describe its status when written; they do not establish later completion.

This selection does not replace the [complete notes index](notes/README.md) or [complete plans index](plans/README.md). Consult the [method documentation](../methods/README.md) for operational definitions. Scores and mechanistic conclusions cannot be carried directly across different models, tokenizers, training budgets, or evaluation sets.

## 1. How Do Architecture and Tokenization Affect Learning from Limited Experience?

1. **Is the benefit due to character composition or simply more parameters?**
   The [surface-structure comparison notes](notes/initial_model_studies/surface_1m_profile_interpretation.md) compare character-fragment composition, an additional lookup table, and an ordinary model using the same example order and shared initialization. The important evidence is the pattern of component-level tradeoffs across two seeds, not a local increase in one column. Follow the [original scores](../experiments/archive/initial_model_studies/data/surface_1m_profile.json) to see whether character sharing outperforms the capacity control.

2. **Which architectural component explains the difference between BERT and DeBERTa?**
   Read the [batch-size and architecture comparison](notes/initial_model_studies/update_geometry_coordinate_comparison.md), followed by the [correction after initializing shared parameters identically](notes/frontier_consolidation/commoncopy_architecture_interaction_result.md). Batch size does not explain the entire difference, but the claim that a positional-scoring component is necessary for compact-rewrite gains was not established either. The [four-condition analysis](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/commoncopy_architecture_interaction_four_cell_bootstrap/architecture_interaction_four_cell_bootstrap.json) distinguishes an architecture's absolute performance from the effect of the data treatment.

3. **Where does whole-word masking improve on token-level masking?**
   The [cross-seed interpretation](notes/initial_model_studies/wwm_cross_seed_stability_interpretation.md) records paired comparisons with the same model, tokenizer, and exposure. The direction of some capability changes repeats across seeds; other capabilities show no consistent advantage. This makes masking granularity a testable training choice, not a universal ranking. Continue to the [first seed's scores](../experiments/archive/initial_model_studies/data/masked_1m_grid_pos512_profile.json), [second seed's scores](../experiments/archive/initial_model_studies/data/masked_1m_seed43_profile.json), and [training program](../experiments/archive/initial_model_studies/scripts/run_masked_1m_grid_pos512.py).

4. **Why does more compact tokenization not necessarily produce a better model?**
   The [tokenizer visibility analysis](notes/representation_and_objectives/strictsmall_tokenizer_summary.md) distinguishes word counts, subword counts, truncation, and visibility of complete pairs. The [interpretation after retraining](notes/representation_and_objectives/corrected_tokenizer_official_vectors_and_route_decision.md) narrows the scope of the early high scores. Inspect the [visibility data](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/strictsmall_tokenizer_visibility_audit/strictsmall_tokenizer_visibility_audit.json) and [two-seed comparison](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/corrected_tokenizer_two_seed_comparison/corrected_tokenizer_two_seed_comparison.json): the corpus used to train the tokenizer is itself an experimental condition.

5. **Can greater depth recover the costs of changing the vocabulary and representation?**
   The [depth-width comparison and decision](notes/representation_and_objectives/depth_vector_decision.md) compares specific recipes with 12 layers at width 384 and 8 layers at width 480. Complete scores do not improve monotonically with depth; gains on some components accompany losses on others. Inspect the [component results](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/depth_vector_decision/depth_vector_decision.json) rather than generalizing a local improvement into a claim that deeper architectures are universally more effective.

## 2. What Budget Does Shortening a Rewrite Actually Free Up?

6. **How can a second wording be constructed without treating a length check as a semantic guarantee?**
   The [rewrite-generation design](notes/relation_learning/rewrite_generation_design_rationale.md) explains the intended pairing, length constraints, and semantic review. The [correction after structural filtering](notes/functional_learning/corrected_real_stream_policy.md) retains counterexamples that changed the meaning without triggering a warning, or simply reverted to the original sentence. Follow the [semantic-judgment program](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/scripts/semantic_judge.py) and [filtered-data construction results](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/structural_policy_materialization/structural_policy_summary.json). These document candidates and checks, not proof that every rewrite is faithful.

7. **How can compression itself be separated from reinvesting the saved words in more sources?**
   The [common-source core and reinvestment design](notes/frontier_consolidation/density_core_reinvestment_medium_riskhard.md) first fixes the sources shared by longer and shorter wordings, then adds more sources to the compact condition. It establishes where the budget comes from and where it goes. Compare the [construction metadata](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/density_core_reinvestment_medium_riskhard/density_core_reinvestment_metadata.json) with the [budget table](../results/compact_budget.csv); compression ratio alone does not establish a model benefit.

8. **If the word counts match on paper, does the model actually read equivalent material?**
   The [construction revision preserving complete rows](notes/frontier_consolidation/density_cleanqwen_overlay_medium_riskhard.md) explains why existing pairs and coherent original text are retained, with only specified complete rows removed. This prevents budget manipulation from simultaneously damaging the background text. Next, inspect the [row-replacement metadata](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/density_cleanqwen_overlay_medium_riskhard/density_cleanqwen_rowholdout_overlay_metadata.json) to check the displaced content, padding words, and actual paired block.

9. **Should the same budget buy more wordings, more independent content, or repetition?**
   The [revised fixed-budget interpretation](notes/frontier_consolidation/fixed_budget_learning_principle_after_review.md) considers what is added, what is sacrificed, and which model and training stage receive the experience. The important shift is away from a single universal "data quality score." Continue to the [register-substitution measurements](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/register_removal_rate_and_reference_spread/register_removal_rate_and_reference_spread.json) and [subsequent two-seed register comparison](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/data/frontier_consolidation_readout/register_seed_contrasts.csv). The proposed interpretive framework is not an already fitted and validated predictive law.

## 3. How Did Residual Branches Evolve from Added Capacity to Frozen-Model Consolidation?

10. **What exactly does zero-output initialization preserve?**
    The [original residual-branch design](notes/frontier_consolidation/adapter_experiment_design.md) starts from an unchanged function at initialization and tests whether the branch can gradually take on learning. Only the [later frozen-parent design](notes/frontier_consolidation/chck82_frozen_private_tail_design.md) fixes the old parameters. Read the [frozen-branch checks](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/frozen82_private_mech_check/frozen82_private_mech_check.json) and [model implementation](../models/frontier/modeling_frozen_slow_private_debertav2.py). Early joint training does not preserve the original function throughout training, and equivalence with the added branch disabled does not guarantee capability preservation when it is enabled.

11. **Why move from auxiliary paired learning to coherent text replay?**
    The [revised frozen-model continuation design](notes/frontier_consolidation/frozen_anchor_fastpath_disruption_design.md) identifies that the old KL-only condition lacked ordinary predictive learning and could not serve as a coherent-replay control. The new comparison retains the objectives and each row's word collection while reordering spans to disrupt longer dependencies. Follow the [replay trainer](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/scripts/frozen82_fastpath_replay_trainer.py) and [late-training control table](../results/late_consolidation_controls.csv). This is a specific text-structure control, not a single-factor comparison covering every earlier condition.

12. **Does improving the score by adjusting the residual scale mean that new capabilities were learned?**
    The [notes separating scale effects from mechanism](notes/frontier_consolidation/private_scale_endpoint_vs_mechanism_synthesis.md) track which items change with scale and test whether confidence rules that do not use the correct answers can distinguish gains from damage. Continue to the [scale results](../results/private_scale_sweep.csv) and [item-level analysis](../experiments/archive/frontier_consolidation/data/globalpiqa_alpha_microaudit/globalpiqa_alpha_microaudit.json). A small number of items can change a component mean; this does not establish broad capability gains.

13. **Does consolidation that works at one mature checkpoint replicate from another starting point?**
    The [decision not to pursue consolidation from an independent starting point](notes/representation_and_objectives/fastpath_closure_and_ordinary84_endpoint.md) records a counterexample: ordinary continued training outperformed the dedicated-branch replay tested there. It preserves the distinction between a useful endpoint and a general consolidation principle. Read the [fixed four-way comparison](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/fastpath80_fourway_readout/task_balanced_readout.json), noting that projected total scores in the original notes used an assumed AoA value and are not the later complete evaluation.

## 4. Which Source Relationship Is Learned, and Which Targets Support It?

14. **Do verbatim repetition and aligned rewrites teach the same way of using a source?**
    The [three-seed neutral-reference analysis](notes/relation_learning/original_threeseed_neutral_anchor.md) separates true sources, unrelated sources, and ordinary neutral text. On the specified compact-rewrite targets, changes in source advantage point in opposite directions for repetition and rewriting. The [consistent results table](../results/relation_context_use.csv) gives seed variability and units. The neutral reference matters because it tests the explanation that only the comparison condition deteriorated; it does not establish that repetition harms every task.

15. **Must both sides of a relationship appear in the same prediction window?**
    The [two-seed same-window and split-window analysis](notes/relation_learning/twoseed_N_anchored_locality_summary.md) preserves each condition's paired materials and budget, separating only their training rows. The large source effects weaken under splitting, supporting a role for local relational practice. Follow the [split-window replication results](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/data/split_seed_replication/) and [neutral-reference data](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/data/split_seed43122_neutral_anchor/). Content is matched within the repetition comparison and within the rewrite comparison, not made identical between repetition and rewriting.

16. **What different target types are hidden within "saying it another way" in natural rewrites?**
    The [correction to the natural-text interpretation](notes/relation_learning/express_scientific_review_and_wikipedia_correction.md) distinguishes target tokens that occur in the source from those entirely absent from it. The [notes on correspondence and objective boundaries](notes/relation_learning/relation_design_and_objective_boundary_integration.md) then compare correct and incorrect pairing and autoregressive objectives. Inspect the [results recomputed by target category](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/data/express_review/wikipedia_transfer_by_target_class.csv) and [source-scoring program](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/scripts/score_paired_context_relation_design.py). Improvement in one category is not general transfer, and the old unmatched split condition is not a strict control for same-window presentation.

17. **Can deleting one category of supervision while preserving the input identify that target category's role?**
    The [source-disjoint target-deletion notes](notes/representation_and_objectives/source_disjoint_target_channel_result.md) compare deleting source-absent content targets with deleting approximately matched copy targets. This separates exposure to a training row from learning a category of predictions, although the interval under stricter document-disjoint evaluation still crosses zero. Continue to the [probe program](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/scripts/source_disjoint_target_type_probe.py), [held-out losses](../results/source_disjoint_target_loss.csv), and [late-training seven-metric results](../results/target_deletion_100m.csv). Rankings on local loss do not automatically determine complete-model rankings.

18. **Does a repetition-rewrite mixture benefit from genuine synergy between the two?**
    The [corrected interpretation of mixed relations](notes/relation_learning/corrected_composition_and_trust.md) withdraws the stronger synergy claim: neutral filler in the half-dose rewrite reference also trains a different predictive relationship. Continue to the [half-dose and mixed-condition readouts](../experiments/archive/relation_learning/data/half_view_curve_probe/), checking absolute true-source loss alongside neutral- and unrelated-source losses. A larger apparent advantage caused by a shifting reference cannot establish a new mechanism of synergy. The unrelated-source interference interpretation is also restricted to similar registers.

## 5. After Familiar Performance Recovers, Can Unseen Symbols Still Access the Same Computation?

19. **Does alternating training help because of timing or the allocation of supervision weights?**
    The [effective-weight matching notes](notes/functional_learning/021b_credit_matched_analysis.md) derive a static weight of 1/17 and compare it with alternating answer supervision and ordinary continued training. The static condition preserves much of the access through unseen symbols, shifting the explanation from "alternation is necessary" toward competition between answer and context learning. Inspect the [matched-analysis data](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/revision_021b_credit_matched/credit_matched_analysis.json). The value 1/17 is an algebraic match for the position counts in this task, not a universal optimum.

20. **Does decodable query information actually control the answer?**
    Read from the [donor-query activation replacement](notes/functional_learning/causal_intervention_analysis.md) to the [correction along the continued-training trajectory](notes/functional_learning/causal_interface_trajectory_analysis.md). Replacing the signal between runs with the same context but different queries can redirect the answer; orthogonal-component controls are also included. Inspect the [replacement program](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/scripts/causal_intervention.py) and [trajectory results](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/causal_interface_trajectory/results.json). Successful replacement supports a functional role for the signal, but does not show that all damage lies in the signal-formation layer or that downstream computation is wholly unchanged.

21. **Does the original correct answer depend on this signal, or does the model respond only when it is forcibly replaced?**
    The [erasure and rotation study during ordinary forward passes](notes/functional_learning/clean_d_component_ablation.md) centers, zeros, or moves the query-matching component across attribute slots. It addresses the dependence question that donor replacement alone cannot answer. Inspect the [intervention results](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/clean_d_component_ablation/results.json) and [program](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/scripts/clean_d_component_ablation.py). The proportion selecting the designated slot after rotation is not accuracy on the original question.

22. **Could failure on unseen symbols simply mean that the information moved to another direction?**
    The [refitted-direction falsification design](notes/functional_learning/held_fitted_direction_test.md) fits directions in the current model separately using familiar queries, a single unseen query, and two unseen queries. Two unseen objects rule out the simple explanation that the model merely finds the unfamiliar slot. Inspect the [fitting and replacement results](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/held_fitted_direction_test/results.json). These linear directions did not recover the original functional reach, but that does not rule out all nonlinear or distributed recodings.

## 6. How Can New Predictive Capabilities Be Acquired While Preserving Function on Ordinary Inputs?

23. **Is hiding the background equivalent to requiring the model to predict it?**
    The [design separating input corruption from loss](notes/functional_learning/corruption_vs_loss_design.md) specifies in advance which interpretations different outcomes would support. The [experiment notes](notes/functional_learning/corruption_vs_loss_synthesis.md) then compare conditions with identically corrupted inputs but different losses. Inspect the [three-condition results](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/corruption_vs_loss_factorial/factorial_summary.json). The cost of background supervision in this templated task is not a universal conclusion about masking or gradients in natural text.

24. **Why can dense masking be paired with sparse supervision?**
    The [sparse-label interpretation correction](notes/relation_learning/sparse_label_interpretation_revision.md) clarifies that targets are content groups selected by rule in the second view, not a single answer position. Additional masked non-target positions change only the available clues. Read the [implementation](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/scripts/densemask_sparselabel_train.py) and [row-by-row verification](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/densemask_sparselabel_row_verify/row_verify.json). A visible source is not necessarily the only way to infer a target; the stronger claim that prediction "must be solved through the source" has been withdrawn.

25. **What exactly does ordinary-state preservation constrain?**
    The [revised preservation-control notes](notes/functional_learning/preservation_controls_and_corrected_interpretation.md) correct the KL direction and identify dropout and random-sequence confounds. The constraint is the divergence from the frozen starting model to the student, and the same complete paired row still contains the source. Follow the computation in the [preservation implementation](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/scripts/clean_preservation_train.py), then inspect the [zero-preservation replay comparison](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/zero_preservation_replay_compare_u1_cpu/zero_preservation_replay_compare.json). Ordinary masking is not an evidence-free state, and the old coefficient comparison was not a pure experiment on preservation strength.

26. **Does the same KL coefficient imply the same preservation pressure?**
    The [evidence-decomposition notes](notes/functional_learning/evidence_decomposition_and_missing_controls.md) compare ordinary and densely masked inputs at identical target positions. The [shared-position gradient diagnostic](../experiments/archive/functional_learning/data/preservation_geometry_matched_diagnostic_full_v2/preservation_geometry_matched_diagnostic.json) shows an approximately twelvefold norm difference. Then inspect the [state-preservation readouts](../results/state_preservation_readouts.csv): a stronger constraint can also suppress newly acquired predictions. The complete conditions additionally change the target-position set, so the effects cannot be attributed solely to input state.

27. **What did the mechanism-informed design ultimately improve, and which controls remain missing?**
    The [follow-up record of the complete two-seed comparison](notes/relation_learning/authoritative_ladder_arithmetic.md) adds ordinary-continuation and acquisition conditions that were still awaiting evaluation in early notes. Compare the [nine-metric results](../results/training_strategy_comparison.csv) with the [mechanistic-claim boundary correction](notes/functional_learning/mechanistic_claim_boundaries.md). Improvements accompany component-level costs, and replication varies continued-training randomness on one shared parent model. Transfer to another parent and a teacher-free control with matched additional presentations remain untested; the gain cannot be independently attributed to the teacher constraint.

## 7. What Do Graph Anchors, Identity Matching, and Selective Reading Each Establish?

28. **Can a few absolute anchors propagate through a relation graph to unanchored relationships?**
    The [multiseed anchor-reversal study and correction](notes/representation_and_objectives/corrected_multiseed_gauge_affine_and_next_raw_binding.md) distinguish closure of relative relations, absolute orientation, and shared representation. Follow the [graph experiment program](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/training/scripts/causal_gauge_probe.py) and [multiseed results](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/multiseed_gauge_affine_full/multiseed_gauge_and_affine_summary.json) to see whether reversing anchors changes unanchored states while preserving relative relationships. Candidates, binary relations, and routing are supplied, so this does not establish general language reasoning.

29. **Can relational computation connect to new names, or does it depend on identities supplied in advance?**
    The [positional-matching repair and equality-learning notes](notes/representation_and_objectives/posalign_repair_and_equality_emergence.md) separate character identity from the relation task, comparing name-level experience with equality supervision covering the basic alphabet. Continue to the [equality pilot data](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/equality_operator_emergence_pilot/) and the [program connecting learned character matching to relations](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/training/scripts/dualpos_learned_equality_gauge_probe.py). This establishes the importance of basic-character coverage, not unsupervised acquisition of addressing for arbitrary natural entities.

30. **Can selecting the right record and reading it be learned separately, then composed?**
    The [selector-reader notes](notes/representation_and_objectives/selector_reader_bridge_synthesis_and_macro_challenge.md) first diagnose the all-negative shortcut in per-candidate binary classification, then train selection with a softmax over four candidates while keeping the reader fixed. Inspect the [composition results](documents/representation_and_objectives/data/selector_reader_groupsoftmax_seed27000/selector_reader_summary.md) and [lexical-matching reference](documents/representation_and_objectives/data/lexical_selector_baseline/lexical_selector_baseline.md). With exact role wording, the composition approaches the upper bound obtained by supplying the correct address, but performance on paraphrases of temporal meaning remains weak.

31. **Why do high scores and write interventions still fail to establish entity binding?**
    Begin with the [withdrawal notice at the top of the small-scale entity-memory notes](notes/representation_and_objectives/entity_memory_miniscreen_synthesis.md), then inspect the [original summary](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/data/miniscreen_synthesis.json) and [revised training program](../experiments/archive/representation_and_objectives/training/scripts/train_entity_memory_v2.py). In the original task, the answer did not change with the queried entity. High scores and sensitivity to write permutations cannot repair that design flaw. The program and corpus have been revised, but this does not mean a revised model has been successfully trained and validated. The withdrawal concerns this inference only.

32. **Which architectural directions remain only candidates?**
    The [post-auxiliary-objective research plan](plans/compact_experience/post_training_plan.md) retains candidates including GEGLU, attention gating, and layer weighting. First inspect the [auxiliary-objective endpoint evaluation](../experiments/archive/compact_experience/data/mlm_mntp_full_eval/per_target/mlm_mntp_aux015_100M.json) that motivated those choices, to understand why changes to representation or architecture were considered. It is not a result for those candidates. The [research catalog](catalog.md) lists gated feed-forward and layer-weighting experiments as not yet run; planned comparisons are not empirical contributions.
